The grammatical terms used by the card popovers throughout this book — parts of speech, cases, tenses, moods, and terms like *declension* and *principal parts* — are defined in the [Latin Reader's Guide](../_language/latin/guide/index.html#grammar), the compact reference shared across the reader, which also covers [pronunciation](../_language/latin/guide/index.html#pronunciation). Click any term inside a card to pop up its definition; each one links onward to the fuller treatment in [Allen &amp; Greenough's New Latin Grammar](../_language/latin/reference/index.html).

Passage-specific commentary for this book will appear here as it is written.
