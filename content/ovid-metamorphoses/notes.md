The grammatical terms used by the card popovers throughout this book — parts of speech, cases, tenses, moods, and terms like *declension* and *principal parts* — are defined in the [Latin Reader's Guide](guide:grammar), the compact reference shared across the reader, which also covers [pronunciation](guide:pronunciation). Click any term inside a card to pop up its definition; each one links onward to the fuller treatment in the [Reference Grammar](ref:contents), after Allen &amp; Greenough's *New Latin Grammar*. The **Study** button at the top of every page opens all of these without leaving the text.

Passage-specific commentary for this book will appear here as it is written.
