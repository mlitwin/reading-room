---
title: Vector fields and flows
---

A **vector field** is a smoothly varying choice of tangent vector at every point. This chapter covers their integral curves and flows and two derivative operators: the Lie bracket of two vector fields, and the Lie derivative along one.

**Where this lands in GR.** A flow is a one-parameter family of transformations. A flow that preserves the metric has a [Killing vector](../11-metric/03-killing-vectors.md) as generator ($\Lie_{\tens{\xi}}\tens{g} = 0$), and by Noether's theorem ([chapter 8](../08-symmetry-and-noether/index.md)) each Killing field gives every free-falling particle a conserved quantity. The energy and angular momentum of Schwarzschild orbits are two of them.
