---
title: Vector fields and flows
---

A **vector field** is a smoothly varying choice of tangent vector at every point. Built on top of vector fields are their integral curves and flows, and the two derivative operators — the Lie bracket of two vector fields, and the Lie derivative along a vector field — that turn vector fields into the basic machinery of dynamical geometry.

**Where this lands in GR.** Flows are one-parameter families of transformations; when a flow preserves the metric, its generator is a [Killing vector](../08-metric/03-killing-vectors.md) — and each Killing field hands every free-falling particle a conserved quantity. The energy and angular momentum that make Schwarzschild orbits tractable are the Lie derivative of chapter 2 doing its GR job.
