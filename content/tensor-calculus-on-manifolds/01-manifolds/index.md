---
title: Manifolds
---

A **smooth manifold** is a topological space that locally looks like $\mathbb{R}^n$ and on which one can do calculus. The four pages here cover the basic objects: the manifold itself, the tangent vectors that live at a point, the cotangent covectors that pair with them, and — a clearly-marked [aside](04-metric-at-a-point.md) — the pointwise metric that will eventually identify the two.

**Where this lands in GR.** Spacetime is a smooth $4$-manifold, and charts are the coordinate systems of physics. That no single chart covers the sphere — let alone a black-hole spacetime — is why GR must be built chart-independent from the start. The aside's Lorentzian tangent space is where special relativity lives, one event at a time.
