title: Manifolds
---

A **smooth manifold** is a topological space that locally looks like $\mathbb{R}^n$ and on which one can do calculus. The four pages cover the manifold itself, the tangent vectors at a point, the covectors that pair with them, and, as a marked [aside](../03-metric/00-metric-at-a-point.md), the pointwise metric that will eventually identify the two.

**Where this lands in GR.** Spacetime is a smooth $4$-manifold, and charts are the coordinate systems of physics. No single chart covers even the sphere, let alone a black-hole spacetime, so GR must be built chart-independently from the start. The aside's Lorentzian tangent space is where special relativity lives, one event at a time.
