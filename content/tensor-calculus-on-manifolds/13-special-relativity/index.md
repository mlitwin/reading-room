---
title: Special relativity
---

Special relativity is the natural-system picture of [chapter 10](../10-lagrangian-mechanics/natural-systems-index.md) with configuration space replaced by spacetime. The kinetic metric becomes the flat Lorentzian metric $\tens{\eta}$, and Newton's absolute time becomes one coordinate among four. It is the flat case that general relativity curves. Each tangent space of a curved spacetime is a copy of it ([the Part I aside](../03-metric/00-metric-at-a-point.md)).

Two pages:

1. [Minkowski spacetime](01-minkowski-spacetime.md): the metric, Lorentz and Poincaré transformations, proper time, four-velocity and four-momentum.
2. [The relativistic particle](02-relativistic-particle.md): the proper-time action, reparametrization invariance, the mass shell, coupling to electromagnetism, and the Newtonian limit.

**Where this lands in GR.** Replace $\tens{\eta}$ by a curved $\tens{g}$. The free-particle action is unchanged in form, and its Euler–Lagrange equations become the geodesic equation.
