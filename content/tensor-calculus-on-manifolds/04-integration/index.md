---
title: Integration
---

Integration on manifolds works on top-degree forms over oriented manifolds. The capstone is Stokes's theorem, which unifies most of the classical integration theorems of vector calculus — the fundamental theorem of calculus, Green's, Gauss's, the classical Stokes — into a single statement about $\int d\omega = \int_\partial \omega$.

**Where this lands in GR.** Integration of forms is what makes the Einstein–Hilbert action $\int_M (R - 2\Lambda)\, \mathrm{vol}_g$ a well-defined number, and Stokes's theorem is behind the integration by parts in every variational derivation and behind conserved-charge integrals over spatial slices.
