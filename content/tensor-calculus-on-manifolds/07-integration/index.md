---
title: Integration
---

Integration on manifolds works on top-degree forms over oriented manifolds. The capstone is [Stokes's theorem](note:stokess-theorem), which unifies most of the classical integration theorems of vector calculus — the fundamental theorem of calculus, Green's, Gauss's, the classical Stokes — into the single statement $\int_M d\tens{\omega} = \int_{\partial M} \tens{\omega}$.

**Where this lands in GR.** Integration of forms is what makes the Einstein–Hilbert action $\int_M (R - 2\Lambda)\, \mathrm{vol}_g$ a well-defined number, and Stokes's theorem is behind the integration by parts in every variational derivation and behind conserved-charge integrals over spatial slices.
