title: Vector fields and flows
---

A **vector field** is a smoothly varying choice of tangent vector at every point. This chapter covers integral curves and flows, and two derivatives: the Lie bracket of two vector fields and the Lie derivative of a tensor field along one.

Three pages:

1. [Vector fields](01-vector-fields.md): sections of $TM$, derivations, pushforward.
2. [Flows and the Lie bracket](02-flows-and-lie-bracket.md): integral curves, flows, $[\tens{X}, \tens{Y}]$.
3. [The Lie derivative](03-lie-derivative.md): $\Lie_{\tens{X}}$ on tensors; $\Lie_{\tens{\xi}} \tens{g} = 0$ and Killing fields.

**Metric-free:** the whole chapter, apart from the Lie derivative of $\tens{g}$.

**Where this lands in GR.** A flow is a one-parameter family of transformations. A flow that preserves the metric is generated by a [Killing vector](../08-connection-and-curvature/03-killing-vectors.md), and by Noether's theorem ([chapter 12](../12-symmetry-and-noether/index.md)) each Killing field gives every free-falling particle a conserved quantity. The energy and angular momentum of Schwarzschild orbits are two of them.

## Notation

| Symbol | Meaning |
|---|---|
| $\tens{X}, \tens{Y}, \tens{Z}$ | vector fields, components $X^i$ |
| $\Lie_{\tens{X}}$ (pound sign) | Lie derivative; $\mathcal{L}$ is kept for Lagrangian densities |
| $[\tens{X}, \tens{Y}]$ | [Lie bracket](note:lie-bracket) |
| $\theta_t$ (theta) | [flow of a vector field](note:flow), on the domain $\mathcal{D}$ |
