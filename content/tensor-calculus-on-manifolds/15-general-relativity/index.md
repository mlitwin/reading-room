General relativity is the geometric theory of gravity. It is built on the geometry of Parts I–II, with the mechanics of Part III and the matter description of [chapter 14](../14-fields-and-stress-energy/index.md) as inputs. Spacetime is a Lorentzian $4$-manifold; matter and energy are described by a stress–energy tensor $T_{\mu\nu}$; the metric is determined by the **[Einstein equations](note:einstein-equations)**, which equate the Einstein tensor (geometry) to a constant times $T_{\mu\nu}$ (matter content).

Five pages:

1. [The Einstein equations](01-einstein-equations.md) and their geometric content.
2. [Schwarzschild](02-schwarzschild.md): the torsion-free worked example, with Kepler alongside.
3. [Einstein–Cartan](03-einstein-cartan.md): gravity with torsion, coupled to spin.
4. [The Newtonian limit](04-newtonian-limit.md): $g_{00} \approx -(1 + 2\Phi)$, Poisson's equation, and tides.
5. [Newton–Cartan gravity](05-newton-cartan.md): Newton's theory as exact spacetime geometry, with gravity as curvature of a connection and no metric.

This is tensor calculus for GR, not a survey of GR: the field equations, one worked solution, a torsionful variant, and the Newtonian limit. Standard textbooks pick up from here.

## Notation

| Quantity | Convention |
|---|---|
| Einstein equations | $G_{\mu\nu} + \Lambda g_{\mu\nu} = 8\pi G\, T_{\mu\nu}$. With the Riemann and Ricci rows, this is MTW's sign class $(+, +, +)$ |
| Units | $c = G = 1$ unless constants are shown |

| Symbol | Meaning |
|---|---|
| $\Lambda$ (Lambda) | cosmological constant |
| $G$ | Newton's constant |
| $M$; $t, r$ | Schwarzschild mass parameter; Schwarzschild coordinates |
| $E$, $L$; $V_{\mathrm{eff}}$ | energy and angular momentum per unit mass; effective potential |
| $\Phi$ (Phi) | Newtonian gravitational potential |
| $h_{\mu\nu}$ | weak-field metric perturbation, $g_{\mu\nu} = \eta_{\mu\nu} + h_{\mu\nu}$ |
| $\tens{\tau}$ (tau), $h^{\mu\nu}$ | Newton–Cartan absolute-time 1-form and degenerate spatial metric |
| $\mathring\Gamma$ | Levi-Civita part of a connection with torsion |
| $K^\rho{}_{\mu\nu}$ | contortion |
| $S^\rho{}_{\mu\nu}$ | spin density |
| $\tens{e}^a$; $\omega^a{}_b$ (omega) | tetrad (orthonormal coframe); spin connection |
