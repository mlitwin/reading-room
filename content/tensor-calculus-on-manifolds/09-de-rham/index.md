---
title: De Rham cohomology
---

The mismatch between [closed and exact forms](note:closed-and-exact-forms) — the kernel of $d$ modulo its image — is itself a topological invariant of the manifold. Two pages: the definition of de Rham cohomology, and the Poincaré lemma, which says the local part of the story is trivial.

**Where this lands in GR.** Nothing later requires this chapter; it is *optional on a first pass*. Read it for why "closed but not exact" is a statement about the topology of spacetime rather than about the fields on it: the closed 1-form with period $2\pi$ on the punctured plane is the prototype for flux integrals, gauge-field topology, and every argument that a global potential fails to exist.
