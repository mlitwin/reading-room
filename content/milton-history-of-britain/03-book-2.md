## The Second Book.

</div>

I Am now to write of what befell the <i><span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="Britons">Britans</span></i> from <i>fifty and three years before the Birth of our <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="Savior">Saviour</span></i>, [when first the <i>Romans</i> came](note:53) in, till the decay and ceasing of that Empire; a story of [much truth](note:muchtruth), and for the first <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="hundred">hunderd</span> years and <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="somewhat">somwhat</span> more, collected without much <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="labor">labour</span>. So many and so prudent were the Writers, which those two, the civilest and the wisest of <i>European Nations</i>, both <i>Italy</i> and <i>Greece</i>, afforded to the actions of that [Puissant <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="City">Citty</span>](note:puissant). For worthy deeds are [not often destitute](note:notoftendestitute) of worthy relaters: as by a certain Fate great Acts and great Eloquence have most commonly <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="gone">gon</span> hand in hand, equalling and <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="honoring">honouring</span> each other in the same Ages. 'Tis true, that in obscurest times, by shallow and <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="unskillful">unskilfull</span> Writers, the indistinct noise of many <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="Battles">Battels</span>, and devastations, of many Kingdoms <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="overrun">over-run</span> and lost, hath come to our <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="Ears">Eares</span>. For what wonder, if in all Ages, Ambition and the love of rapine hath <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="stirred">stirr'd</span> up greedy and violent men to bold attempts in wasting and ruining <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="Wars">Warrs</span>, which to posterity have left the work of Wild Beasts and Destroyers, rather <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="than">then</span> the Deeds and Monuments of men and <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="Conquerors">Conquerours</span>. But he whose just and true <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="valor">valour</span> uses the necessity of <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="War">Warr</span> and Dominion not to destroy, but to prevent destruction, to bring in liberty against Tyrants, Law and Civility among barbarous Nations, knowing that when he Conquers all things else, he cannot Conquer <i>Time</i> or <i>Detraction</i>, wisely conscious of this his want as well as of his worth not to be <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="forgotten">forgott'n</span> or <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="concealed">conceal'd</span>, <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="honors">honours</span> and hath recourse to the aid of Eloquence, his <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="friendliest">freindliest</span> and best supply; by whose immortal Record his noble deeds, which else were transitory, becoming <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="fixed">fixt</span> and durable againt the force of <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="Years">Yeares</span> and Generations, he fails not to continue through all Posterity, over <i>Envy</i>, <i>Death</i>, and <i>Time</i>, also victorious. <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="Therefore">Therfore</span> when the esteem of Science, and liberal study waxes low in the <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="Commonwealth">Common-wealth</span>, <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="we">wee</span> may presume that also there all civil <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="Virtue">Vertue</span>, and worthy action is grown as low to a decline: and then Eloquence, as it were consorted in the same destiny, with the decrease and fall of <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="virtue">vertue</span>, corrupts also and fades; at least <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="resigns">resignes</span> her office of relating to <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="illiterate">illiterat</span> and frivolous Historians; such as the persons <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="themselves">themselvs</span> both <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="deserve">deserv</span>, and are best <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="pleased">pleas'd</span> with; whilst they want either the understanding to choose better, or the innocence to dare invite the examining, and searching <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="style">stile</span> of an intelligent and <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="faithful">faithfull</span> Writer to the <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="survey">survay</span> of <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="their">thir</span> unsound exploits, better <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="befriended">befreinded</span> by obscurity <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="than">then</span> Fame. As for these, the only Authors <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="we">wee</span> have of <i><span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="British">Brittish</span></i> matters, while the power of <i>Rome</i> <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="reached">reach'd</span> hither, (for [<i>Gildas</i> affirms](note:gildasaffirms2) that of the <i>Roman</i> times <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="no">noe</span> <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="British"><i>Brittish</i></span> Writer was in his <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="days">daies</span> extant, or if any ever were, either burnt by Enemies, or transported with such as fled the <i>Pictish</i> and <i>Saxon</i> invasions) these <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="therefore">therfore</span> only <i>Roman</i> Authors there <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="be">bee</span>, who in the [English Tongue](note:englishtongue) have laid together, as much, and perhaps more <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="than">then</span> was requisite to a History of <i>Britain</i>. So that were it not for leaving an unsightly gap so <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="near">neer</span> to the beginning, I should have <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="judged">judg'd</span> this <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="labor">labour</span>, <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="wherein">wherin</span> so little seems to be <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="required">requir'd</span> above transcription, almost superfluous. <i>[Julius Cæsar](note:juliusauthor)</i> (of whom, and of the <i>Roman</i> Free State, more <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="than">then</span> what appertains, is not here to be <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="discoursed">discours'd</span>) having <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="subdued">subdu'd</span> most part of [<i>Gallia</i>](note:gaul), which by a potent faction, he had <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="obtained">obtain'd</span> of the <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="Senate">Senat</span> as his Province for many years, <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="stirred">stirr'd</span> up with a desire of adding still more glory to his name, and the whole <i>Roman</i> Empire to his ambition; [<span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="some">som</span> say](note:som), with a <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="far">farr</span> meaner and ignobler, the Desire of <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="British"><i>Brittish</i></span> Pearls, whose bigness he delighted to <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="balance">ballance</span> in his hand, <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="determines">determins</span>, and that upon no unjust pretended occasion, to <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="try">trie</span> his Force in the Conquest also of <i>Britain</i>. For except Merchants and Traders, it is not oft, saith he, that any use to Travel <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="thither">thether</span>; and to those that <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="do">doe</span>, besides the Sea Coast, and the Ports next to <i>Gallia</i>, nothing else is known. [But <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="here">heer</span>](note:known) I must require, as <i>Pollio</i> did, the diligence, at least the memory of <i>Cæsar:</i> for if it were true, as they of <i>Rhemes</i> told him, that [<i>Divitiacus</i>](note:diviat), not long before a Puissant King of the <i>Soissons</i>, had <i>Britain</i> also under his Command, besides the <i>Belgian Colonies</i> which he affirms to have <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="named">nam'd</span>, and <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="peopled">peopl'd</span> many Provinces there, if also the <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="Britons"><i>Britans</i></span> had so frequently <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="given">giv'n</span> them aid in all <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="their">thir</span> <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="Wars">Warrs</span>, if lastly, the <i>Druid</i> learning <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="honoured"><span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="honored">honour'd</span></span> so much among them, were at first taught them out of <i>Britain</i>, and they who soonest would attain that Discipline, sent <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="hither">hether</span> [to learn](note:tolearn); it appears not how <i>Britain</i> at that time should be so utterly <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="unknown">unknow'n</span> in <i>Gallia</i>, or only <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="given">know'n</span> to Merchants, yea to them so little, that being <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="called">call'd</span> together from all parts, none could be found to inform <i>Cæsar</i> of what bigness the <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="Isle">Ile</span>, what Nations, how great, what use of <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="War">Warr</span> they had, what Laws, or so much as what commodious Havens for bigger Vessels.
