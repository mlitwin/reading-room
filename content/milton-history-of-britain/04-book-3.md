## The Third Book.

</div>

THis third Book having to tell of accidents as various and exemplary as the intermission or change of Government hath any where brought forth, may deserve attention more than common, and repay it with like benefit to them who can judiciously read: considering especially that the [late civil broils](note:civilwar) had cast us into a condition not much unlike to what the <i><span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="Britons">Britans</span></i> then were in, when the imperial jurisdiction departing hence left them to the sway of <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="their">thir</span> own Councils; which times by comparing seriously with these later, and that confused Anarchy with this <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="interreign">[intereign](note:intereign)</span>, we may be able from two such remarkable turns of State, producing like events among us, to raise a <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="knowledge">knowledg</span> of our selves both great and weighty, by judging hence what kind of men the <i><span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="Britons">Britans</span></i> generally are in matters of so high enterprise, how by nature, industry, or custom fitted to attempt or <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="undergo">undergoe</span> matters of so main consequence: for if it be a high point of wisdom in every private man, much more is it in a Nation to know it self; rather than <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="puffed">puft</span> up with vulgar flatteries, and encomiums, for want of self-knowledge, to enterprise rashly and come off miserably in [great undertakings](note:digression1681). The <i><span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="Britons">Britans</span></i> thus as we heard being left without protection from the Empire, and the Land in a manner emptied of all her youth, consumed in <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="Wars">Warrs</span> abroad, or not caring to return home, themselves through long subjection, [servile in mind](note:servileinmind), <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="slothful">sloathful</span> of body, and with the use of Arms unacquainted, <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="sustained">sustain'd</span> but ill for many years the violence of those [barbarous Invaders](note:malmesbury), who now daily grew upon them. For although at first greedy of change, and to be thought the [leading Nation](note:leadingnation) to freedom from the Empire, they <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="seemed">seem'd</span> a while to <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="bestir">bestirr</span> them with a <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="show">shew</span> of diligence in <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="their">thir</span> new affairs, <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="some">som</span> secretly aspiring to rule, others adoring the name of liberty, yet so soon as they felt by proof the weight of what it was to govern well themselves, and what was wanting within them, not stomach or the love of licence, but the wisdom, the virtue, the <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="labor">labour</span>, to use and maintain true <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="liberty">libertie</span>, they soon remitted <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="their">thir</span> heat, and shrunk more wretchedly under the burden of <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="their">thir</span> own <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="liberty">libertie</span>, than before under a <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="foreign">foren</span> yoke. Insomuch that the residue of those <i>Romans</i> which had planted themselves <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="here">heer</span>, despairing of <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="their">thir</span> ill deportment at home, and weak resistance in the field by those few who had the courage or the strength to bear Arms, nine years after the sacking of <i>Rome</i> <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="removed">remov'd</span> [out of <i>Britain</i>](note:outofbritain) into <i>France</i>, [hiding for haste](note:hidingforhaste) great part of <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="their">thir</span> treasure, which was never after found. And now again the <i><span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="Britons">Britans</span></i>, no longer able to support themselves against the prevailing Enemy, <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="solicit">sollicit</span> <i>Honorius</i> to <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="their">thir</span> aid, with mournful Letters, Embassages [and vows](note:andvows) of perpetual subjection to <i>Rome</i>, if the <i>Northern</i> Foe were but <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="repulsed">repuls't</span>. He at <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="their">thir</span> request [spares them](note:sparesthem) one Legion, which with great slaughter of the <i>Scots</i> and <i>Picts</i> drove them beyond the Borders, <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="rescued">rescu'd</span> the <i><span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="Britons">Britans</span></i>, and <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="advised">advis'd</span> them to [build a Wall](note:greatslaughter) cross the <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="Island">Iland</span>, between Sea and Sea, from the place where [<i><span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="Edinburgh">Edinburg</span></i> now stands](note:edinburgnowstands) to the <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="Firth">Frith</span> of <i><span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="Dunbarton">Dunbritton</span></i>, by the City <i>Alcluith</i>. But the material being only Turf, and by the rude multitude unartificially built up without better direction, <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="availed">avail'd</span> them little. For no sooner was the [Legion departed](note:legiondeparted), but the greedy spoilers returning, land in great numbers from <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="their">thir</span> Boats and <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="Pinnaces">Pinaces</span>, wasting, slaying, and treading down all before them. Then are messengers again posted to <i>Rome</i> in lamentable sort, beseeching that they would not suffer a whole Province to be <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="destroyed">destroy'd</span>, and the <i>Roman</i> name, so <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="honorable">honourable</span> yet among them, to become the subject of barbarian scorn and insolence. [The Emperor](note:speedwaspossible), at <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="their">thir</span> sad complaint, with what speed was possible, sends to <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="their">thir</span> <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="succor">succour</span>. Who coming suddenly on those ravenous multitudes that minded only spoil, surprise them with a terrible slaughter. They who <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="escaped">escap'd</span> fled back to those Seas, from whence yearly they were wont to arrive, and return <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="laden">lad'n</span> with booties. But the <i>Romans</i> who came not now to rule, but charitably to aid, declaring that it stood not longer with the ease of <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="their">thir</span> Affairs to make such laborious voyages in pursuit of so base and vagabond robbers, of whom neither glory was to be got, nor gain, exhorted them to manage <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="their">thir</span> own warfare; and to defend like men <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="their">thir</span> Country, <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="their">thir</span> Wives, <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="their">thir</span> Children, and what was to be dearer than life, <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="their">thir</span> liberty, against an Enemy not stronger than themselves, if <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="their">thir</span> own sloth and <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="speedwaspossible">cowardise</span> had not made them so; if they would but only find hands to grasp defensive Arms, rather than basely stretch them out to <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="receive">receave</span> bonds. They gave them also <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="their">thir</span> [help to build](note:helptobuild) a new Wall, not of earth as the former, but of stone, (both at the [public cost](note:publiccost), and by particular contributions) traversing the <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="Isle">Ile</span> in direct line from East to West, between certain Cities <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="placed">plac'd</span> there as Frontiers to bear off the Enemy, where <i>Severus</i> had <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="walled">wall'd</span> once before. They <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="raised">rais'd</span> it 12 Foot high, 8 broad. Along the South <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="shore">shoar</span>, because from thence also like hostility was <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="feared">fear'd</span>, they place Towers by the Sea side at certain distances, for safety of the Coast. Withall they instruct them in the Art of <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="War">Warr</span>, leaving Patterns of <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="their">thir</span> Arms and Weapons behind them; and with animating words, and many lessons of <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="valor">valour</span> to a faint-hearted audience, bid them finally farewell, without purpose to return. And these two friendly Expeditions, the last of any hither by the <i>Romans</i>, were <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="performed">perform'd</span>, as may be <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="gathered">gatherd</span> out of <i>Beda</i> and <i>Diaconus</i>, the two last years of <i>Honorius</i>. <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="Their">Thir</span> Leader, as <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="some">som</span> modernly write, was [<i>Gallio</i>](note:gallio) of <i>Ravenna</i>; <i>Buchanan</i>, who [departs not](note:hispredecessor) much from the Fables of his Predecessor <i>Boethius</i>, names him <i>Maximianus</i>, and brings against him to this <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="Battle">Battel</span> <i>Fergus</i> first King of <i>Scots</i>, after <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="their">thir</span> second <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="supposed">suppos'd</span> coming into <i>Scotland</i>, <i>Durstus</i> King of <i>Picts</i>, both there slain, and <i>Dioneth</i> an imaginary King of <i>Britain</i>, or Duke of <i>Cornwall</i>, who <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="improbably">improbablie</span> sided with them against his own <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="Country">Countrie</span>, <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="hardly">hardlie</span> escaping. With [no less exactness](note:nolessexactness) of particular circumstances, he takes upon him to relate all those <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="tumultuary">tumultuarie</span> <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="inroads">inrodes</span> of the <i>Scots</i> and <i>Picts</i> into <i>Britain</i>, as if they had but yesterday <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="happened">happen'd</span>, thir order of <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="Battle">Battel</span>, manner of fight, number of slain, Articles of Peace, things whereof <i>Gildas</i> and <i>Beda</i> are utterly silent, Authors to whom the <i>Scotch</i> Writers have none to cite comparable in Antiquity; no more therefore to be <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="believed">believ'd</span> for bare assertions, however <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="quaintly">quaintlie</span> <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="dressed">drest</span>, than our <i><span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="Geoffrey">Geofrey</span></i> of <i>Monmouth</i> when he varies most from <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="authentic">authentick</span> <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="story">storie</span>. But either the inbred vanity of some, in that respect unworthily <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="called">call'd</span> Historians, or the fond zeal of praising <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="their">thir</span> Nations above truth, hath so far transported them, that where they find nothing faithfully to relate, they fall confidently to invent what they think may either best set off <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="their">thir</span> <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="History">Historie</span>, or <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="magnify">magnifie</span> <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="their">thir</span> <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="Country">Countrie</span>.

The <i>Scots</i> and <i>Picts</i> in manners differing <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="somewhat">somwhat</span> from each other, but still unanimous to rob and <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="spoil">spoile</span>, hearing that the <i>Romans</i> intended not to return, from <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="their">thir</span> [Gorroghs](note:gorroghs), or Leathern <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="Frigates">Frigats</span> pour out themselves [in swarms](note:inswarms) upon the Land, more confident than ever; and from the North end of the <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="Isle">Ile</span> to the very wall side, then first took possession as inhabitants; while the <i><span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="Britons">Britans</span></i> with idle Weapons in <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="their">thir</span> hands stand trembling on the Battlements, till the half-naked <i>Barbarians</i> with <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="their">thir</span> long and formidable Iron hooks pull them down headlong. The rest not only quitting the wall but Towns and Cities, leave them to the <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="bloody">bloodie</span> pursuer, who follows killing, wasting, and destroying all in his way. From these confusions arose a <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="Famine">Famin</span>, and from thence discord and civil commotion among the <i><span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="Britons">Britans</span></i>: each man living by what he <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="robbed">rob'd</span> or took violently from his <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="Neighbor">Neighbour</span>. When all stores were <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="consumed">consum'd</span> and spent where men inhabited, they betook them to the Woods, and <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="lived">liv'd</span> by hunting, which was <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="their">thir</span> only sustainment. To the heaps of these evils from without, were added [new divisions](note:theseevils) within the Church. For <i>Agricola</i> the Son of <i>Severianus</i> a <i>Pelagian</i> Bishop had spread his Doctrine wide among the <i><span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="Britons">Britans</span></i> not uninfected before. The sounder part neither willing to embrace his opinion to the overthrow of divine grace, nor able to refute him, crave assistance from the Churches of <i>France</i>: who send them <i>Germanus</i> [Bishop of <i>Auxerre</i>](note:bishopofauxerre), and <i>Lupus</i> of <i>Troyes</i>. They by [continual preaching](note:continualpreaching) in Churches, in Streets, in Fields, and not without miracles, as is <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="written">writt'n</span>, <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="confirmed">confirm'd</span> <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="some">som</span>, <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="regained">regain'd</span> others, and at <i>Verulam</i> in a [public disputation](note:publicdisputation) put to silence <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="their">thir</span> chief adversaries. This reformation in the Church was <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="believed">beleev'd</span> to be the cause of <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="their">thir</span> success a while after in the field. For the <i>Saxons</i> and <i>Picts</i> with [joint force](note:jointforce), which was no new thing before the <i>Saxons</i> at least had any dwelling in this <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="Island">Iland</span>, during the abode of <i>Germanus</i> <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="here">heer</span>, had made a strong impression from the North. The <i><span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="Britons">Britans</span></i> [marching out](note:marchingout) against them, and mistrusting <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="their">thir</span> own power, send to <i>Germanus</i> and his <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="Colleague">Collegue</span>, reposing more in the spiritual strength of those two men, than in <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="their">thir</span> own thousands <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="armed">arm'd</span>. They came, and <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="their">thir</span> presence in the Camp was not less than if a whole Army had <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="come">com</span> to second them. It was then the time of <i>Lent</i>, and the people instructed by the daily Sermons of these two Pastors, came flocking to <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="receive">receave</span> Baptism. There was a place in the Camp set apart as a Church, and <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="tricked">trick'd</span> up with boughs upon <i>Easter-day</i>. The Enemy understanding this, and that the <i><span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="Britons">Britans</span></i> were <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="taken">tak'n</span> up with Religions more than with feats of Arms, advances, after the Paschal Feast, as to a certain <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="Victory">Victorie</span>. <i>German</i>, who also had intelligence of <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="their">thir</span> approach, undertakes to be Captain that day; and riding out with selected Troops to discover what advantages the place might offer, lights on a Valley <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="compassed">compass't</span> about with Hills, by which the Enemy was to pass. And placing there his ambush, warns them, that what word they heard him pronounce aloud, the same they should repeat with universal shout. The Enemy passes on securely, and <i>German</i> thrice aloud cries <i><span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="Hallelujah">Halleluia</span></i>; which answered by the <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="Soldiers">Souldiers</span> with a <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="sudden">sudd'n</span> burst of <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="clamor">clamour</span>, is from the Hills and Valleys redoubled. The <i>Saxons</i> and <i>Picts</i> on a sudden supposing it the noise of a huge <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="Host">Hoast</span>, throw themselves into flight, casting down <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="their">thir</span> Arms, and great numbers of them are <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="drowned">drown'd</span> in the River which they had newly <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="passed">pass'd</span>. This Victory, thus won without hands, left to the <i><span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="Britons">Britans</span></i> plenty of <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="spoil">spoile</span>, and the person and the preaching of <i>German</i> greater authority and reverence than before. And the exploit might pass for current, if <i>Constantius</i>, the Writer of his life in the next age, had <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="resolved">resolv'd</span> us how the <i>British</i> Army came to want baptizing; for of any Paganism at that time, or long before, in the Land we read not, or that [<i>Pelagianism</i>](note:pelagianismdef) was <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="re-baptised">re-baptiz'd</span>. The place of this Victory, [as is reported](note:asisreported), was in <i>Flintshire</i> by a Town <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="called">call'd</span> <i>Guid-cruc</i>, and the [river <i>Allen</i>](note:alyn), where a field retains the name of <i>Maes German</i> to this day. But so soon as <i>German</i> [was <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="returned">return'd</span> home](note:wasreturndhome), the <i>Scots</i> and <i>Picts</i>, though now so many of them Christians, that <i>[Palladius](note:nowsomany)</i> a Deacon was <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="ordained">ordain'd</span> and sent by <i>Celestine</i> the Pope to be a Bishop over them, were [not so well](note:notsowell) <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="reclaimed">reclaim'd</span>, or not so many of them as to cease from doing mischief to <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="their">thir</span> <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="Neighbors">Neighbours</span>, where they found no impeachment to fall in yearly as they were wont. They therefore of the <i><span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="Britons">Britans</span></i> who perhaps were not yet wholly <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="ruined">ruin'd</span>, in the strongest and South-west parts of the <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="Isle">Ile</span>, [send Letters](note:letterstoaetius) to <i>Ætius</i>, then third time Consul of <i>Rome</i>, with this [superscription](note:superscription); <i>To <span class="roman">Ætius</span> thrice Consul, the groanes of the <span class="roman"><span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="Britons">Britans</span></span></i>. And after a [few words](note:fewwords) thus: <i>The barbarians drive us to the Sea, the Sea drives us back to the barbarians; thus bandied up and down between two deaths, we perish either by the Sword or by the Sea.</i> But the Empire at that time overspread with <i>Hunns</i> and <i>Vandals</i>, was not in condition to lend them aid. Thus rejected and wearied out with continual flying from place to place, but more afflicted with Famine, which then grew outrageous among them, many for hunger yielded to the Enemy, others either more resolute, or less <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="exposed">expos'd</span> to wants, keeping within Woods, and Mountainous places, not only defended themselves, but sallying out at length gave a stop to the insulting Foe with many seasonable defeats; led by [some eminent person](note:eminentperson), as may be thought, who exhorted them not to trust in <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="their">thir</span> own strength, but in Divine assistance. And perhaps no other <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="here">heer</span> is meant than the foresaid deliverance by <i>German</i>, if computation would permit, which <i>Gildas</i> either not much regarded, or might mistake; but that he tarried so long <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="here">heer</span>, the Writers of his life [assent not](note:assentnot). Finding therefore such opposition, the <i>Scots</i> or <i>Irish</i> Robbers, for so they are indifferently <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="termed">term'd</span>, without delay get them home. The <i>Picts</i>, as before was mentioned, then first began to settle in the utmost parts of the <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="Island">Iland</span>, using now and then to make <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="inroads">inrodes</span> upon the <i><span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="Britons">Britans</span></i>. But they in the mean while thus <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="rid">ridd</span> of <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="their">thir</span> Enemies, begin afresh to till the ground; which after cessation yields her fruit in such abundance, as had not formerly <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="been">bin</span> known for many Ages. But wantonness and luxury, the wonted companions of plenty, grow up as fast, and with them, if <i>Gildas</i> [deserve belief](note:deservebelief), all other vices incident to human corruption. That which he notes especially to be the chief perverting of all good in the Land, and so continued in his days, was the hatred of truth, and all such as durst appear to vindicate and maintain it. Against them, as against the only disturbers, all the malice of the Land was bent. Lies and falsities, and such as could best invent them, were only in request. Evil was <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="embraced">embrac'd</span> for good, wickedness <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="honored">honour'd</span> and <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="esteemed">esteem'd</span> as virtue. And this quality <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="their">thir</span> <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="valor">valour</span> had, against a <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="foreign">foren</span> Enemy to be ever backward and heartless; to civil broils eager and prompt. In matters of Government, and the search of truth, weak and shallow, in <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="falsehood">falshood</span> and wicked deeds pregnant and industrious. Pleasing to God, or not pleasing, with them weighed alike; and the worse [most an end](note:mostanend) was the weightier. All things were done contrary to public welfare and safety; nor only by secular men, for the Clergy also, whose Example should have guided others, were as <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="vicious">vitious</span> and corrupt.
