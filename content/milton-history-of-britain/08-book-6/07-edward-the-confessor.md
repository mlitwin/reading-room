### <i>Edward</i> the Confessor.

</div>

GLad were the English <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="delivered">deliverd</span> so unexpectedly from <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="their">thir</span> Danish <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="Masters">Maisters</span>, and little thought how <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="near">neer</span> another Conquest was hanging over them. <i>Edward</i>, the [<i>Easter</i> following](note:1043), <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="Crowned">Crown'd</span> at <i>Winchester</i>, the same year [accompanied](note:accompanied) with Earl <i>Godwin</i>, <i>Leofric</i> and <i>Siward</i>, came again <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="thither">thether</span> on a sudden, and by <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="their">thir</span> Counsel <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="seized">seis'd</span> on the treasure of his Mother <i>Emma</i>. The [next year](note:1048) <i>Harold Harvager</i> King of <i>Norway</i> sending <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="Ambassadors">Embassadors</span>, [made peace](note:madepeacewith) with King <i>Edward</i>; but an Earthquake at <i><span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="Worcester">Worster</span></i> and <i><span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="Derby">Darby</span></i>, Pestilence and <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="Famine">Famin</span> in many places, much <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="lessened">less'nd</span> the enjoyment <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="thereof">therof</span>. The King <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="therefore">therfore</span> with a great Navy coming to <i>Sandwich</i>, there <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="stayed">staid</span> till the <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="Emperor">Emperour</span> came to an agreement with Earl <i>Baldwin</i>. But that which gave beginning to many troubles <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="ensuing">ensueing</span>, <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="happened">happ'nd</span> this year, and upon [this occasion](note:thisoccasion). <i>Eustace</i> Earl of <i><span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="Bologne">Boloign</span></i>, Father of the famous <i>Godfrey</i> who won <i>Jerusalem</i> from the Saracens, and Husband to <i>Goda</i> the <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="King&#x27;s">Kings</span> Sister, having <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="been">bin</span> to visit King <i>Edward</i>, and returning by <i>Canterbury</i> to take Ship at <i>Dover</i>, one of his Harbingers insolently seeking to lodge by force in a House there, <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="provoked">provok'd</span> so the Master <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="thereof">therof</span>, as by chance or heat of anger to kill him.
