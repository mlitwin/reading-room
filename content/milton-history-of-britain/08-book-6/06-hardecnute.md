### <i>Hardecnute.</i>

</div>

<i>HArdecnute</i> <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="received">receav'd</span> with acclamation, and [seated in the Throne](note:hardecnutecrowned), first <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="called">call'd</span> to mind the injuries done to him or his Mother <i>Emma</i> in the time of <i>Harold</i>; sent [<i>Alfric</i>](note:aelfric) Archbishop of <i><span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="York">Yorke</span></i>, <i>Godwin</i> and others, with <i>Troud</i> his Executioner to <i>London</i>, commanding them to dig up the body of King <i>Harold</i>, and throw it into a Ditch; but by a second order, into the <i>Thames</i>. Whence <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="taken">tak'n</span> up by a Fisherman, and <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="conveyed">convei'd</span> to a Church-yard in <i>London</i> belonging to the Danes, it was <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="interred">enterr'd</span> again with <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="honor">honour</span>. This done, he levied a sore Tax, that 8 marks to every Rower, and twelve to every Officer in his Fleet should be paid throughout <i>England</i>: by which time they who were so forward to call him over, had <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="enough">anough</span> of him; for he, as they thought, had too much of theirs. After this he <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="called">call'd</span> to account <i>Godwin</i> Earl of <i>Kent</i>, and <i>Leving</i> Bishop of <i><span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="Worcester">Worster</span></i>, about the Death of <i><span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="Alfred">Elfred</span></i> his half Brother, which <i>Alfric</i> the Archbishop laid to <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="their">thir</span> charge; the King <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="deprived">depriv'd</span> <i>Leving</i> of his <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="Bishopric">Bishoprick</span>, and gave it to his accuser: but the year following, <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="pacified">pacifi'd</span> with a [round <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="sum">summe</span>](note:roundsumme) <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="restored">restor'd</span> it to <i>Leving</i>. <i>Godwin</i> made his peace by a sumptuous present, a <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="Galley">Gally</span> with a <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="gilded">guilded</span> stem bravely <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="rigged">rigg'd</span>, and 80 <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="Soldiers">Souldiers</span> in her, every one with Bracelets of gold on each Arm, weighing 16 ounces, Helmet, Corslet, and Hilts of his Sword <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="gilded">guilded</span>; a Danish Curtax listed with gold or silver, hung on his left shoulder, a <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="Shield">Sheild</span> with boss and <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="nails">nailes</span> <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="gilded">guilded</span> in his left hand, in his right a <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="Lance">Launce</span>; besides this, he took his Oath before the King, that neither of his own <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="counsel">councel</span> or will, but by the command of <i>Harold</i> he had done what he did, to the putting out <i><span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="Alfred&#x27;s">Elfreds</span></i> Eyes. The like Oath took most of the Nobility for themselves, or in his behalf. The [next year](note:1041) <i>Hardecnute</i> sending his Housecarles, so they <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="called">call'd</span> his Officers, [to gather](note:togather) the Tribute <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="imposed">impos'd</span>; two of them rigorous in <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="their">thir</span> Office, were slain at <i><span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="Worcester">Worster</span></i> by the people; <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="whereat">wherat</span> the King <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="enraged">enrag'd</span> sent <i>Leofric</i> Duke of <i>Mercia</i>, and <i>Seward</i> of <i>Northumberland</i>, with great Forces and Commission to slay the <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="Citizens">Cittizens</span>, rifle and burn the City, and <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="waste">wast</span> the whole Province. Affrighted with such news, all the people fled: the Countrymen whither they could, the <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="Citizens">Cittizens</span> to a small <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="Island">Iland</span> in <i>Severn</i>, <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="called">call'd</span> <i>Beverege</i>, which they <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="fortified">fortifi'd</span> and defended stoutly till peace was granted them, and freely to return home. But <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="their">thir</span> City they found <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="sacked">sack't</span> and <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="burned">burnt</span>; wherwith the King was <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="appeased">appeas'd</span>. This was commendable in him, however cruel to others, that towards his half brethren, though Rivals of his Crown, he <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="showed">shew'd</span> himself <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="always">alwayes</span> tenderly <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="affectioned">affectiond</span>; as now towards <i>Edward</i>, who without fear came to him out of <i>Normandy</i>, and with unfeigned kindness <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="received">receav'd</span>, <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="remained">remain'd</span> safely and <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="honorably">honourably</span> [in his Court](note:1042). But <i>Hardecnute</i> the year following, [at a Feast](note:atafeast) <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="wherein">wherin</span> <i>Osgod</i> a great Danish Lord gave his Daughter in marriage at <i>Lambeth</i>, to <i>Prudon</i> another potent Dane; in the midst of his mirth, sound and <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="healthful">healthfull</span> to sight, while he was drinking fell down speechless, and so dying, was buried at <i>Winchester</i> beside his Father. He was it seems a great lover of good <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="cheer">chere</span>; sitting at Table <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="four">fowr</span> times a day, with great variety of Dishes and superfluity to all <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="Comers">Commers</span>. <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="Whereas">Wheras</span>, [saith <i>Huntingdon</i>](note:saithhuntingdonfood), in our time Princes in <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="their">thir</span> houses made but one meal a day. He gave his Sister <i>Gunildis</i>, a Virgin of rare Beauty, [in marriage](note:inmarriage) to <i>Henry</i> the <i>Alman</i> <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="Emperor">Emperour</span>; and to send her forth pompously, all the Nobility contributed <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="their">thir</span> Jewels and richest Ornaments. But it may seem a wonder that our Historians, if they deserve that name, should in a matter so remarkable, and so <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="near">neer</span> <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="their">thir</span> own time, so much differ. [<i>Huntingdon</i> relates](note:huntingdonmarriageerror) against the credit of all other records, that <i>Hardecnute</i> thus dead, the English <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="rejoicing">rejoycing</span> at this unexpected riddance of the Danish yoke, sent over to <i><span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="Alfred">Elfred</span></i> the Elder Son of <i>Emma</i> by King <i>Ethelred</i>, of whom we heard but now, that he <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="died">dy'd</span> a <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="Prisoner">Prisner</span> at <i><span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="Ely">Eely</span></i>, sent <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="thither">thether</span> by <i>Harold</i> six year before; that he came now out of <i>Normandy</i>, with a great number of men, to <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="receive">receave</span> the Crown; that Earl <i>Godwin</i> aiming to have his Daughter Queen of <i>England</i> by marrying her to <i>Edward</i> a simple youth, for he thought <i><span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="Alfred">Elfred</span></i> of a higher Spirit <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="than">then</span> to accept her, persuaded the Nobles that <i><span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="Alfred">Elfred</span></i> had brought over too many <i>Normans</i>, had <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="promised">promis'd</span> them lands <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="here">heer</span>, that it was not safe to suffer a Warlike and <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="subtle">suttle</span> Nation to take root in the Land, that these were to be so <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="handled">handl'd</span> as none of them might dare for the future to flock hither, upon pretence of relation to the King: <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="thereupon">therupon</span> by common consent of the Nobles, both <i><span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="Alfred">Elfred</span></i> and his Company were dealt with as was above related; that they then sent for <i>Edward</i> out of <i>Normandy</i>, with hostages to be left there of <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="their">thir</span> <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="faithful">faithfull</span> intentions to make him King, and <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="their">thir</span> desires not to bring over with him many <i>Normans</i>; that <i>Edward</i> at <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="their">thir</span> call came then first out of <i>Normandy</i>; <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="whereas">wheras</span> all others agree that he came voluntarily over to visit <i>Hardecnute</i>, as is before said, and was remaining then in Court at the time of his Death. For [<i>Hardecnute</i> dead](note:hardecnutedead), saith <i><span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="Malmesbury">Malmsbury</span></i>, <i>Edward</i> doubting greatly his own safety, <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="determined">determin'd</span> to rely wholly on the advice and <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="favor">favour</span> of Earl <i>Godwin</i>; desiring <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="therefore">therfore</span> by messengers to have <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="private">privat</span> speech with him, the Earl a while deliberated: at last assenting, Prince <i>Edward</i> came, and would have <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="fallen">fall'n</span> at his feet; but that not permitted, told him the danger <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="wherein">wherin</span> he thought himself at present, and in great perplexity besought his help to convey him some <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="whither">whether</span> out of the Land. <i>Godwin</i> soon apprehending the fair occasion that now as it were prompted him how to advance himself and his Family, <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="cheerfully">cherfully</span> exhorted him to remember himself the Son of <i>Ethelred</i>, the Grandchild of <i>Edgar</i>, right Heir to the Crown, at full Age; not to think of flying, but of reigning, which might easily be brought about if he would follow his Counsel; then setting forth the power and authority which he had in <i>England</i>, <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="promised">promisd</span> it should be all his to set him on the Throne, of he on his part would promise and swear to be for ever his friend, to preserve the <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="honor">honour</span> of his House, and to marry his Daughter. <i>Edward</i>, as his necessity then was, consented easily, and swore to whatever <i>Godwin</i> <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="required">requir'd</span>. An Assembly of States <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="thereupon">therupon</span> met at <i>Gillingham</i>, where <i>Edward</i> pleaded his right; and by the <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="powerful">powerfull</span> influence of <i>Godwin</i> was accepted. [Others](note:asbromton), as <i><span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="Brompton">Bromton</span></i>, with no probability write, that <i>Godwin</i> at this time was fled into <i><span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="Denmark">Denmarke</span></i>, for what he had done to <i><span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="Alfred">Elfred</span></i>, <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="returned">return'd</span> and submitted himself to <i>Edward</i> then King, was by him <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="charged">charg'd</span> <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="openly">op'nly</span> with the Death of <i><span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="Alfred">Elfred</span></i>, and not without much ado, by the intercession of <i>Leofric</i> and other Peers, <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="received">receav'd</span> at length into <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="favor">favour</span>.
