### <i>Ethelred.</i>

</div>

Whereat <i>Dunstan</i> much <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="troubled">troubl'd</span>, for he stood by and saw it, to them next him broke into [these words](note:thesewordssluggard), By God and <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="God&#x27;s">Gods</span> Mother this Boy will prove a Sluggard. Another thing is <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="written">writt'n</span> of him in his Childhood; which <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="argued">argu'd</span> no bad nature, that hearing of his Brother <i><span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="Edward&#x27;s">Edwards</span></i> cruel Death, he made loud lamentation; but his furious mother offended <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="therewith">therwith</span>, and having no rod at hand, beat him so with great Wax Candles, that he hated the sight of them ever after. <i>Dunstan</i> though unwilling set the Crown upon his head; but at the same time foretold <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="openly">op'nly</span>, as is reported, the great evils that were to come upon him and the Land, in <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="avengement">avengment</span> of his <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="Brother&#x27;s">Brothers</span> innocent blood. And about the [same time](note:abouttime), one midnight, a Cloud sometimes bloody, sometimes fiery, was seen over all <i>England</i>; and within [three years](note:982) the <i>Danish</i> [Tempest](note:tempest), which had long <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="surceased">surceast</span>, <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="revolved">revolv'd</span> again upon this <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="Island">Iland</span>. To the more ample relating whereof, the <i>Danish</i> History, at least <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="their">thir</span> latest and [diligentest Historian](note:diligentesthistorian), as neither from the first landing of <i>Danes</i>, in the Reign of <i>West-Saxon</i> <i>Brithric</i>, so now again from first to last, contributes nothing; busied more <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="than">then</span> <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="enough">anough</span> to make out the bare names and successions of <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="their">thir</span> uncertain Kings, and <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="their">thir</span> small actions at home: unless out of him I should transcribe what <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="he">hee</span> takes, and I better may, from our own Annals; the surer and the sadder witnesses of <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="their">thir</span> doings here, not glorious, as they vainly boast, but most inhumanly Barbarous. For the <i>Danes</i> well [understanding](note:wellunderstanding), that <i>England</i> had now a <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="slothful">slothfull</span> King to <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="their">thir</span> wish, first landing at <i>Southampton</i> from 7 great Ships, took the Town, <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="spoiled">spoil'd</span> the Country, and carried away with them great pillage; nor was <i>Devonshire</i> and <i>Cornwall</i> [uninfested](note:uninfested) on the shore; <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="Pirates">Pirats</span> of <i>Norway</i> also harried the Coast of <i>West-chester</i>: and to add a worse calamity, the [City of <i>London</i>](note:casually) was <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="burned">burnt</span>, casually or not, is not <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="written">writt'n</span>. It <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="chanced">chanc'd</span> <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="four">fowr</span> [years after](note:986), that <i>Ethelred</i> <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="besieged">beseig'd</span> <i>Rochester</i>, some way or other offended by the Bishop <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="thereof">therof</span>. <i>Dunstan</i> [not approving](note:thecause) the cause, sent to warn him that he provoke not <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="Saint">St.</span> <i>Andrew</i> the Patron of that City, nor <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="waste">wast</span> his Lands; an old craft of the Clergy to secure <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="their">thir</span> Church Lands, by entailing them on some Saint; the King not <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="hearkening">hark'ning</span>, <i>Dunstan</i> on this condition that the <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="siege">seige</span> might be <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="raised">rais'd</span>, sent him an <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="hundred">hunderd</span> pound, the money was accepted and the <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="siege">seige</span> <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="dissolved">dissolv'd</span>. <i>Dunstan</i> reprehending his avarice, sent him again this word, because thou hast respected money more <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="than">then</span> Religion, the evils which I foretold shall the sooner come upon thee; but not in my days, for so God hath <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="spoken">spok'n</span>. The [next year](note:993) but one they [took by storm](note:tookbystorm) and <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="rifled">rifl'd</span> <i>Bebbanburg</i> an <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="ancient">antient</span> City nigh <i>Durham</i>: sailing thence into the mouth of <i>Humber</i>, they wasted both sides <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="thereof">therof</span>, <i><span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="Yorkshire">Yorkeshire</span></i> and <i>Lindsey</i>, burning and destroying all before them. Against these went out three Noblemen, <i>Frana</i>, <i>Frithegist</i>, and <i>Godwin</i>; but being all <i>Danes</i> by the <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="Father&#x27;s">Fathers</span> side, willingly began flight, and forsook <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="their">thir</span> own Forces <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="betrayed">betray'd</span> to the Enemy. The King for this treason of <i>Alfric</i>, put out his <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="Son&#x27;s">Sons</span> Eyes; but the <i>Londoners</i> both by land and water, so valiantly resisted <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="their">thir</span> <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="besiegers">beseigers</span>, that they were <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="forced">forc't</span> in one day with great loss to give over.
