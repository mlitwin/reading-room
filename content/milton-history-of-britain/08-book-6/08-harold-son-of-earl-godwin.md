### [<i>Harold</i>](note:harold2) Son of Earl <i>Godwin.</i>

</div>

<i>Harold</i>, whether by King <i>Edward</i> a [little before](note:littlebefore) his Death <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="ordained">ordain'd</span> Successor to the Crown, as <i>Simeon</i> of <i>Durham</i>, and others affirm; or by the prevalence of his faction, excluding <i>Edgar</i> the right Heir, Grandchild to <i>Edmund</i> <i>Ironside</i>, as <i><span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="Malmesbury">Malmsbury</span></i> and <i>Huntingdon</i> [agree](note:malmesburyagree), no sooner was the Funeral of King <i>Edward</i> ended, but on the same day was elected and <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="Crowned">Crown'd</span> King: and no sooner <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="placed">plac't</span> in the Throne, but began to frame himself by all manner of compliances to gain affection, <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="endeavored">endeavour'd</span> to make good Laws, <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="repealed">repeal'd</span> bad, became a great Patron to Church and Church-men, courteous and affable to all reputed good, a hater of <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="evil">evill</span> doers, <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="charged">charg'd</span> all his Officers to punish <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="Thieves">Theeves</span>, Robbers, and all disturbers of the peace, while he himself by Sea and Land <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="labored">labourd</span> in the <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="defense">defence</span> of his Country: so good an actor is ambition. In the mean while a [blazing Star](note:blazingstar), 7 mornings together, about the end of <i>April</i> was seen to stream terribly, not only over <i>England</i>, but other parts of the World; foretelling <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="here">heer</span>, as was thought, the great changes approaching: plainliest prognosticated by <i>Elmer</i> a Monk of <i><span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="Malmesbury">Malmsbury</span></i>, who could not foresee, when time was, the breaking of his own Legs for soaring too high. He in his youth strangely aspiring, had made and fitted Wings to his Hands and Feet; with these on the top of a Tower, spread out to gather air, he flew more <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="than">then</span> a Furlong; but the wind being too high, came fluttering down, to the maining of all his Limbs; yet so conceited of his Art, that he attributed the cause of his fall to the want of a Tail, as Birds have, which he forgot to make to his hinder parts. This story, though seeming otherwise too light in the midst of a sad narration, yet for the <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="strangeness">strangness</span> <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="thereof">therof</span>, I thought worthy <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="enough">anough</span> the <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="placing">placeing</span> as I found it <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="placed">plac't</span> in [my <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="Author">Authour</span>](note:myauthour). But to digress no <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="farther">farder</span>, [<i>Tosti</i>](note:tostibrother) the <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="King&#x27;s">Kings</span> Brother coming from <i>Flanders</i>, full of Envy at his younger <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="Brother&#x27;s">Brothers</span> advancement to the Crown, <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="resolved">resolv'd</span> what he might to trouble his Reign; forcing <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="therefore">therfore</span> them of <i>Wight <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="Isle">Ile</span></i> to contribution, he <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="sailed">sail'd</span> thence to <i>Sandwich</i>, committing Piracies on the Coast between. <i>Harold</i> then residing at <i>London</i>, with a great number of Ships drawn together, and of Horse Troops by Land, prepares in person for <i>Sandwich</i>: <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="whereof">wherof</span> <i>Tosti</i> having notice, directs his course with [60 Ships](note:60ships) towards <i>Lindsey</i>, taking with him all the Sea-men he found, willing or unwilling: where he <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="burned">burnt</span> many Villages, and slew many of the Inhabitants; but [<i>Edwin</i>](note:edwinofmercia) the <i>Mercian</i> Duke, and <i>Morcar</i> his Brother, the <i>Northumbrian</i> Earl, with <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="their">thir</span> Forces on either side, soon drove him out of the Country. Who thence betook him to <i>Malcolm</i> the Scottish King, and with him abode the whole Summer. About the same time Duke <i>William</i> sending <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="Ambassadors">Embassadors</span> to admonish <i>Harold</i> of his promise and Oath, to assist him in his Plea to the Kingdom, he made answer, that by the death of his Daughter betroth'd to him on [that condition](note:thatcondition), he was <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="absolved">absolv'd</span> of his Oath, or not Dead, he could not take her now an out-landish woman, without consent of the Realm; that it was presumptuously done, and not to be persisted in, if without consent or knowledge of the States, he had sworn away the right of the <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="Kingdom">Kingdome</span>; that what he swore was to gain his liberty, being in a manner then his <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="Prisoner">Prisner</span>; that it was unreasonable in the Duke to require or expect of him the forgoing of a <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="Kingdom">Kingdome</span>, <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="conferred">conferr'd</span> upon him with universal <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="favor">favour</span> and acclamation of the people: to this flat <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="denial">deniall</span> he added contempt, sending the Messengers back, saith <i><span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="Matthew">Mathew</span> Paris</i>, on <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="maimed">maim'd</span> Horses. The Duke thus contemptuously put off, addresses himself to the Pope, setting forth the Justice of his cause, which <i>Harold</i>, whether through haughtiness of mind, or distrust, or that the ways to <i>Rome</i> were <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="stopped">stop'd</span>, sought not to do. Duke <i>William</i>, besides the Promise and Oath of <i>Harold</i>, <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="alleged">alleg'd</span> that King <i>Edward</i> by the advice of <i><span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="Siward">Seward</span></i>, <i>Godwin</i> himself, and <i>Stigand</i> the Archbishop, had <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="given">giv'n</span> him the right of succession, and had sent him the Son and Nephew of <i>Godwin</i>, pledges of the <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="gift">guift</span>; the Pope sent to Duke <i>William</i>, after this demonstration of his right, a consecrated Banner. <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="Whereupon">Wherupon</span> he having with great care and choice got an Army of tall and stout <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="Soldiers">Souldiers</span>, under Captains of great skill and mature Age, came in <i>August</i> to the Port of <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="Saint">St.</span> <i>Valerie</i>. But ere the middle of <i>September</i>, provision failing when it was most needed, both Fleet and Army return home. When on a sudden, <i>Harold <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="Hardrada">Harvager</span></i> King of <i>Norway</i>, [with a Navy](note:withanavy) of more <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="than">then</span> 500 great Ships, (others <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="lessen">less'n</span> them by two <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="hundred">hunderd</span>, others augment them to a thousand) appears at the mouth of <i>Tine</i>; to whom Earl <i>Tosti</i> with his Ships came as was agreed between them; whence both uniting, set sail with all speed and <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="entered">enterd</span> the River <i>Humber</i>. Thence turning into <i>Ouse</i>, as far as <i>Rical</i>, landed; and won <i><span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="York">Yorke</span></i> by assault. At these <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="tidings">tideings</span> <i>Harold</i> with all his power <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="hastes">hasts</span> <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="thitherward">thetherward</span>; but ere his coming, <i>Edwin</i> and <i>Morcar</i> at <i>Fulford</i> by <i><span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="York">Yorke</span></i>, on the North side of <i>Ouse</i>, about the Feast of <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="Saint">St.</span> <i><span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="Matthew">Mathew</span></i> had <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="given">giv'n</span> them <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="Battle">Battel</span>; successfully at first, but over-born at length with numbers; and <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="forced">forc't</span> to turn <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="their">thir</span> backs, more of them <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="perished">perish'd</span> in the River, <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="than">then</span> in the Fight. While these things <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="passed">past</span> in <i>Northumberland</i>, Duke <i>William</i> lay still at <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="Saint">St.</span> <i>Valerie</i>; his Ships were <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="ready">readie</span>, but the wind <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="served">serv'd</span> not for many days; which put the <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="Soldiery">Souldierie</span> into much discouragement and murmur, taking this for an unlucky sign of <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="their">thir</span> success; at last the wind came <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="favorable">favourable</span>, the Duke first under sail awaited the rest at Anchor, till all coming forth, the whole Fleet of 900 Ships with a [prosperous gale](note:prosperousgale) <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="arrived">arriv'd</span> at <i>Hastings</i>.
