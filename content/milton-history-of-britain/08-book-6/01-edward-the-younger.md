### <i>Edward</i> the Younger.

</div>

<i>EDward</i> the [eldest Son](note:eldestson) of <i>Edgar</i> by <i>Egelfleda</i> his first Wife, the Daughter of Duke <i>Ordmer</i>, was according to right and his <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="Father&#x27;s">Fathers</span> Will <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="placed">plac'd</span> in the Throne; <i>Elfrida</i> his second Wife, and her faction only <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="repining">repineing</span>, who <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="labored">labour'd</span> to have had her Son <i>Ethelred</i> a Child of 7 years, <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="preferred">preferr'd</span> before him; that she under that <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="pretense">pretence</span> might have <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="ruled">rul'd</span> all. Mean while Comets were seen in <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="Heaven">Heav'n</span>, portending not <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="Famine">Famin</span> only, which <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="followed">follow'd</span> the next year, but the <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="troubled">troubl'd</span> State of the whole Realm not long after to ensue. The troubles begun in <i>Edwi's</i> <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="days">daies</span>, between Monks and secular Priests, now <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="revived">reviv'd</span> and drew on either side many of the Nobles into parties. For <i>Elfere</i> Duke of the <i>Mercians</i>, with many other Peers, corrupted as is said with <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="gifts">guifts</span>, [drove the Monks](note:drovethemonks) out of those Monasteries where <i>Edgar</i> had <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="placed">plac'd</span> them, and in <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="their">thir</span> stead put secular Priests with <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="their">thir</span> Wives. But <i>Ethelwin</i> Duke of <i>East-Angles</i>, with his Brother <i>Elfwold</i>, and Earl <i>Britnoth</i> <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="opposed">oppos'd</span> them, and gathering an Army defended the <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="Abbeys">Abbies</span> of <i>East-Angles</i> from such intruders. To appease these tumults, a Synod was <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="called">call'd</span> at <i>Winchester</i>, and nothing there concluded, a general <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="Council">Councel</span> both of Nobles and Prelates was held [at <i>Caln</i>](note:caln) in <i>Wiltshire</i>, where while the dispute was hot, but chiefly against <i>Dunstan</i>, the room <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="wherein">wherin</span> they sat fell upon <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="their">thir</span> heads, killing some, maiming others, <i>Dunstan</i> only escaping upon a beam that fell not, and the King absent by reason of his tender Age. This accident quieted the <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="controversy">controversie</span>, and brought both parts to hold with <i>Dunstan</i> and the Monks. Mean while the King addicted to a Religious life, and of a mild Spirit, simply permitted all things to the ambitious will of his Step-mother and her Son <i>Ethelred</i>: to whom she <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="displeased">displeas'd</span> that the name only of King was wanting, <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="practiced">practis'd</span> thenceforth to remove King <i>Edward</i> out of the way; which in this manner she brought about. <i>Edward</i> on a day wearied with hunting, thirsty and alone, while his attendance <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="followed">follow'd</span> the Dogs, hearing that <i>Ethelred</i> and his mother <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="lodged">lodg'd</span> at <i>Corvesgate</i> ([<i>Corfe</i> Castle](note:corsecastle), saith <i>Camden</i>, in the <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="Isle">Ile</span> of <i>Purbeck</i>) innocently went <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="thither">thether</span>. She with all <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="show">shew</span> of kindness welcoming him, commanded drink to be brought forth, for it seems he lighted not from his Horse; and while he was drinking, <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="caused">caus'd</span> one of her Servants, privately before instructed, to stab him with a <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="poniard">poignard</span>. The poor youth, who little expected such unkindness there, turning speedily the Reins, fled bleeding; till through loss of blood falling from his Horse, and expiring, yet held with one foot in the <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="Stirrup">Stirrop</span>, he was <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="dragged">dragg'd</span> along the way, <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="traced">trac'd</span> by his blood, and buried without <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="honor">honour</span> at <i>Werham</i>, having <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="reigned">reign'd</span> about [3 years](note:978): but the place of his burial not long after grew famous for miracles. After which by Duke <i>Elfer</i> (who, as <i><span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="Malmesbury">Malmsbury</span></i> saith, [had a hand](note:grewfamous) in his Death) he was Royally <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="interred">enterr'd</span> at <i>Skepton</i> or <i>Shaftsbury</i>. The <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="murderess">murdress</span> <i>Elfrida</i> at length repenting, spent the residue of her <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="days">daies</span> in sorrow and great penance.
