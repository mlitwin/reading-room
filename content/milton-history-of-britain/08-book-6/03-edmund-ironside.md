### <i>Edmund Ironside.</i>

</div>

AFter [the decease](note:thedecease) of <i>Ethelred</i>, they of the Nobility who were then at <i>London</i>, together [with the Citizens](note:withthecitizens), chose <i>Edmund</i> his Son (not by <i>Emma</i>, but a former Wife the Daughter of Earl <i>Thored</i>) in his <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="Father&#x27;s">Fathers</span> room; but the Archbishops, Abbots, and many of the Nobles assembling together, elected <i>Canute</i>; and coming to <i><span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="Southampton">Southamton</span></i> where he then <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="remained">remain'd</span>, <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="renounced">renounc'd</span> before him all the race of <i>Ethelred</i>, and swore him fidelity: he also swore to them, in matters both religious and secular, to be <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="their">thir</span> <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="faithful">faithfull</span> Lord. But <i>Edmund</i> [with all speed](note:withallspeed) going to the West-<i>Saxons</i>, was joyfully <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="received">receav'd</span> of them as <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="their">thir</span> King, and of many other Provinces by their example. Mean while <i>Canute</i> about mid <i>May</i> came with his whole Fleet up the River to <i>London</i>; then causing a great [Dike](note:dike) to be made on <i>Surrey</i> side, <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="turned">turn'd</span> the stream, and drew his Ships <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="thither">thether</span> West of the Bridge; then begirting the City with a broad and deep trench, <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="assailed">assail'd</span> it on every side; but <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="repulsed">repulst</span> as before by the valorous Defendants, and in despair of success at that time, leaving part of his Army for the <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="defense">defence</span> of his Ships, with the rest sped him to the <i>West-Saxons</i>, ere <i>Edmund</i> could have time to assemble all his powers: who yet with such as were at hand invoking divine aid, <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="encountered">encounterd</span> the <i>Danes</i> at <i>Pen</i> by <i>Gillingham</i> in <i>Dorsetshire</i>, and put him to flight. After mid-summer, <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="increased">encreast</span> with new Forces, he met with him again at a Place <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="called">call'd</span> <i>Sherastan</i>, now <i>Sharstan</i>; but <i>Edric</i>, <i>Almar</i>, and <i>Algar</i>, with the <i><span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="Hampshire">Hamshire</span></i> and <i>Wiltshire</i> men, then <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="siding">sideing</span> with the <i>Danes</i>, he only <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="maintained">maintain'd</span> the fight, <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="obstinately">obstinatly</span> fought on both sides, till night and weariness parted them. Day light returning <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="renewed">renu'd</span> the conflict; wherein the <i>Danes</i> appearing <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="inferior">inferiour</span>, <i>Edric</i> to <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="dishearten">dishart'n</span> the English cuts off the Head of one <i>Osmer</i>, in <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="countenance">countnance</span> and hair somewhat resembling the King, and holding it up, cries aloud to the English, that <i>Edmund</i> being slain and this his head, it was time for them to <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="fly">flie</span>; which <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="fallacy">falacy</span> <i>Edmund</i> <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="perceiving">perceaving</span>, and <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="openly">op'nly</span> <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="showing">shewing</span> himself to his <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="Soldiers">Souldiers</span>, by a [spear thrown](note:spearthrown) at <i>Edric</i>, that missing him yet slew one next him, and through him another <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="behind">behinde</span>, they <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="recovered">recoverd</span> heart, and lay sore upon the <i>Danes</i> till night parted them as before: for ere the third morn, <i>Canute</i> sensible of his loss, <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="marched">march'd</span> away by stealth to his Ships at <i>London</i>, <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="renewing">renuing</span> there his [leagre](note:leagre). Some would have this <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="Battle">Battel</span> at <i>Sherastan</i> the same with that at <i>Scorastan</i> before <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="mentioned">mention'd</span>, but the circumstance of time permits not that, having <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="been">bin</span> before the landing of <i>Canute</i>, this a good while after, as by the Process of things appears. From <i>Sherastan</i> or <i>Sharstan</i>, <i>Edmund</i> <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="returned">return'd</span> to the <i>West-Saxons</i>, whose <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="valor">valour</span> <i>Edric</i> fearing, <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="lest">least</span> it might prevail against the <i>Danes</i>, sought pardon of his revolt, and obtaining it, swore loyalty to the King, who now the third time coming with an Army from the <i>West-Saxons</i> to <i>London</i>, <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="raised">rais'd</span> the <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="Siege">Seige</span>, <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="chasing">chaseing</span> <i>Canute</i> and his <i>Danes</i> to <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="their">thir</span> Ships. Then after two <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="days">daies</span> passing the <i>Thames</i> at [<i>Branford</i>](note:branford), and so coming on <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="their">thir</span> backs, kept them so <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="turned">turn'd</span>, and <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="obtained">obtain'd</span> the Victory: then returns again to his <i>West-Saxons</i>, and <i>Canute</i> to his <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="Siege">Seige</span>, but still in vain; <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="rising">riseing</span> <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="therefore">therfore</span> thence, he <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="entered">enterd</span> with his Ships a River then <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="called">call'd</span> <i>Arenne</i>; and from the Banks <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="thereof">therof</span> wasted <i>Mercia</i>; thence <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="their">thir</span> Horse by land, <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="their">thir</span> Foot by Ship came to <i>Medway</i>. <i>Edmund</i> in the mean while with <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="multiplied">multipli'd</span> Forces out of many Shires crossing again at <i>Branford</i>, came into <i>Kent</i>, seeking <i>Canute</i>; <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="encountered">encounterd</span> him at <i>Otford</i>, and so defeated, that of his Horse, they who <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="escaped">escap'd</span> fled to the <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="Isle">Ile</span> of <i>Sheppey</i>; and a full Victory he had <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="gained">gain'd</span>, had not <i>Edric</i> still the <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="Traitor">Traytor</span> by some wile or other <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="detained">detain'd</span> his <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="pursuit">persuit</span>: and <i>Edmund</i> who never wanted courage, <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="here">heer</span> wanted prudence to be so misled, ever after <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="forsaken">forsak'n</span> of his wonted Fortune. <i>Canute</i> crossing with his Army into <i>Essex</i>, thence wasted <i>Mercia</i> worse <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="than">then</span> before, and with heavy prey <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="returned">return'd</span> to his Ships: them <i>Edmund</i> with a collected Army <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="pursuing">persueing</span>, overtook at a place <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="called">call'd</span> <i>Assandune</i> or <i>Asseshill</i>, [now <i>Ashdown</i>](note:nowashdown) in <i>Essex</i>; the <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="Battle">Battel</span> on either side was fought with great vehemence; but perfidious <i>Edric</i> <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="perceiving">perceaving</span> the Victory to incline towards <i>Edmund</i>, with that part of the Army which was under him, fled, as he had <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="promised">promis'd</span> <i>Canute</i>, and left the King <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="overmatched">over-match't</span> with numbers: by which desertion the English were overthrown, Duke <i>Alfric</i>, Duke <i>Godwin</i>, and <i>Ulfketel</i> the valiant Duke of <i>East-Angles</i>, with a great part of the Nobility slain, so as the English of a long time had not <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="received">receav'd</span> a greater blow. Yet after a while <i>Edmund</i> not absurdly <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="called">call'd</span> <i>Ironside</i>, preparing to try again his Fortune in another <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="field">feild</span>, was <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="hindered">hinderd</span> by <i>Edric</i> and others of his faction, <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="advising">adviseing</span> him to make peace and divide the <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="Kingdom">Kingdome</span> with <i>Canute</i>. To which <i>Edmund</i> <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="overruled">over-rul'd</span>, a treaty appointed, and pledges mutually <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="given">giv'n</span>, both Kings met together at a place <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="called">call'd</span> [<i>Deorhirst</i>](note:anddivide) in <i><span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="Glocestershire">Glostershire</span></i>; <i>Edmund</i> on the West side of <i>Severn</i>, <i>Canute</i> on the East with <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="their">thir</span> Armies, then both in person wafted into an <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="Island">Iland</span>, [at that time](note:atthattime2) <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="called">call'd</span> <i>Olanege</i>, now <i>Alney</i>, in the midst of the River; swearing amity and brotherhood, they parted the <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="Kingdom">Kingdome</span> between them. Then interchanging <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="Arms">Armes</span> and the habit they wore, assessing also what pay should be allotted to the Navy, they departed each his way. Concerning this <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="interview">interveiw</span> and the cause <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="thereof">therof</span>, others write otherwise; <i><span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="Malmesbury">Malmsbury</span></i>, that <i>Edmund</i> <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="grieving">greiving</span> at the loss of so much blood spilt for the ambition only of two men <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="striving">striveing</span> who should reign, of his own accord sent to <i>Canute</i>, offering him [single <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="Combat">Combate</span>](note:singlecombate), to prevent in <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="their">thir</span> own cause the effusion of more blood <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="than">then</span> <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="their">thir</span> own; that <i>Canute</i>, though of courage <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="enough">anough</span>, yet not unwisely doubting to adventure his body of small Timber, against a man of Iron sides, <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="refused">refus'd</span> the <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="Combat">Combate</span>, <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="offering">offring</span> to divide the <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="Kingdom">Kingdome</span>; this offer pleasing both Armies, <i>Edmund</i> was not difficult to consent; and the decision was, that he as his hereditary <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="Kingdom">Kingdome</span> should rule the <i>West-Saxons</i> and all the <i>South</i>, <i>Canute</i> the <i>Mercians</i>, and the <i>North</i>. <i>Huntingdon</i> <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="followed">follow'd</span> by <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="Matthew">Mat.</span> <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="Westminster">West.</span> [relates](note:westrelates), that the Peers on every side wearied out with <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="continual">continuall</span> warfare, and not refraining to affirm <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="openly">op'nly</span>, that they two who expected to reign singly, had most reason to fight singly, the Kings were content; the <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="Island">Iland</span> was their lists, the <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="Combat">Combate</span> Knightly; till <i><span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="Canute">Knute</span></i> finding himself too weak, began to <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="parley">parle</span>, which ended as is said before. After which the <i>Londoners</i> bought <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="their">thir</span> peace of the <i>Danes</i>, and permitted them to winter in the City. But King <i>Edmund</i> about the Feast of <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="Saint">St.</span> <i>Andrew</i>, unexpectedly <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="deceased">deceas'd</span> at <i>London</i>, and was buried <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="near">neer</span> to <i>Edgar</i> his Grandfather at <i>Glaston</i>. The cause of his so sudden death is uncertain; [common fame](note:commonfame), saith <i><span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="Malmesbury">Malmsbury</span></i>, <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="lays">laies</span> the guilt <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="thereof">therof</span> upon <i>Edric</i>, who to please <i>Canute</i>, <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="allured">allur'd</span> with promise of reward two of the <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="King&#x27;s">Kings</span> Privy Chamber, though at first abhorring the fact, to assassinate him at the stool, by thrusting a sharp Iron into his hinder parts. <i>Huntingdon</i>, and <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="Matthew">Mat.</span> <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="Westminster">West.</span> [relate it done](note:relateitdone) at <i>Oxford</i> by the Son of <i>Edric</i>, and something vary in the manner, not worth recital. <i>Edmund</i> dead, <i>Canute</i> meaning to reign sole King of <i>England</i>, calls to him all the Dukes, Barons, and Bishops of the Land, cunningly demanding of them who were witnesses what agreement was made between him and <i>Edmund</i> dividing the <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="Kingdom">Kingdome</span>, whether the Sons and Brothers of <i>Edmund</i> were to govern the <i>West-Saxons</i> after him, <i>Canute</i> living? they who understood his meaning, and <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="feared">fear'd</span> to undergo his anger, timorously <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="answered">answerd</span>, that <i>Edmund</i> they knew had left no part <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="thereof">therof</span> to his Sons or Brethren, living or dying; but that he intended <i>Canute</i> should be <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="their">thir</span> Guardian, till they came to age of reigning. [<i>Simeon</i> affirms](note:simeonaffirmsthat), that for fear or hope of reward they attested what was not true: notwithstanding which, he put many of them to death not long after.
