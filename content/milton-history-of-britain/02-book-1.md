## THE HISTORY OF BRITAIN,

### BOOK I.

</div>

THe beginning of Nations, those excepted of whom sacred Books have <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="spoken">spok'n</span>, is to this day unknown. Nor only the beginning, but the deeds also of many succeeding Ages, yea periods of Ages, either wholly unknown, or <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="obscured">obscur'd</span> and <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="blemished">blemisht</span> with Fables. Whether it were that the use of Letters came in long after, or were it the violence of barbarous inundations, or they themselves at certain revolutions of time, fatally decaying, and degenerating into Sloth and Ignorance; <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="whereby">wherby</span> the monuments of more ancient civility have <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="been">bin</span> <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="some">som</span> <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="destroyed">destroy'd</span>, <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="some">som</span> lost. Perhaps dis-esteem and contempt of the public affairs then present, as not worth recording, might partly be in cause. Certainly oft-times we see that wise men, and of best <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="ability">abilitie</span>, have <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="forborne">forborn</span> to write the Acts of <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="their">thir</span> own <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="days">daies</span>, while they beheld with a just loathing and disdain, not only how unworthy, how <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="perverse">pervers</span>, how corrupt, but often how ignoble, how petty, how below all History the persons and <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="their">thir</span> actions were; who either by fortune, or <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="some">som</span> rude election had <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="attained">attain'd</span> as a sore judgment and <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="ignominy">ignominie</span> upon the Land, to have <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="chief">cheif</span> sway in managing the Commonwealth. What ever might be the reason, this we find, that of <i>British affairs</i>, from the first peopling of the <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="Island">Iland</span> to the coming of <i>Julius <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="Caesar">Cæsar</span></i>, nothing certain, either by Tradition, History, or Ancient Fame hath hitherto <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="been">bin</span> left us. That which we have [of oldest seeming](note:berossus), hath by the greater part of judicious Antiquaries <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="been">bin</span> long rejected for a modern Fable.

Nevertheless there being others, besides the first <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="supposed">suppos'd</span> Author, men not unread, nor <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="unlearned">unlerned</span> in <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="Antiquity">Antiquitie</span>, who <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="admit">admitt</span> that for approved story, which the former explode for fiction, and seeing that oft-times relations <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="heretofore">heertofore</span> accounted fabulous have <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="been">bin</span> after found to contain in them many footsteps, and <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="relics">reliques</span> of <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="something">somthing</span> true, as what we [read in Poets](note:poetsofflood) of the Flood, and Giants little <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="believed">beleev'd</span>, <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="until"><span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="til"><span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="&#x27;til">till</span></span></span> undoubted witnesses taught us, that all was not <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="feigned">fain'd</span>; I have <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="therefore">therfore</span> <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="determined">determin'd</span> to bestow the telling over <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="even">ev'n</span> of these reputed Tales; be it for nothing else but in <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="favor">favour</span> of our English Poets, and Rhetoricians, who by <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="their">thir</span> Art will know, how to use them judiciously.

I might also produce example, as <i>[Diodorus](note:diodorus)</i> among the <i>Greeks</i>, <i><span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="Livy">[Livie](note:livie)</span></i> and others of the <i><span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="Latins">Latines</span></i>, <i>[Polydore](note:polydore)</i> and <i>Virunnius</i> accounted among our own Writers. But I intend not with controversies and quotations to delay or interrupt the smooth course of History; much less to argue and debate long who were the first Inhabitants, with what probabilities, what authorities each opinion hath <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="been">bin</span> upheld, but shall <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="endeavor">endevor</span> that which hitherto hath <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="been">bin</span> needed most, with plain, and <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="lightsome">lightsom</span> brevity, to relate well and orderly things worth the noting, so as may best instruct and benefit them that read. Which, imploring divine assistance, that it may redound to his glory, and the good of the <i>British</i> Nation, <i>I now begin.</i>

That the whole Earth was inhabited [before the Flood](note:beforeflood), and to the utmost point of habitable ground, from those effectual words of <i>God</i> in the Creation, may be more <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="than">then</span> <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="conjectured">conjectur'd</span>. Hence that this <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="Island">Iland</span> also had her dwellers, her affairs, and perhaps her stories, <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="even">eev'n</span> in that old World those many <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="hundred">hunderd</span> years, with much reason we may <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="infer">inferr</span>. So that this outlandish figment may easily excuse our not allowing it the room <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="here">heer</span> so much as of a <i>British</i> Fable.

That which follows, perhaps as wide from truth, though seeming less impertinent, is, that these <i>Samotheans</i> under the Reign of <i>Bardus</i> were <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="subdued">subdu'd</span> by <i>[Albion](note:albiongiant)</i> a Giant, Son of <i>Neptune</i>; who <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="called">call'd</span> the <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="Island">Iland</span> after his own name, and <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="ruled">rul'd</span> it 44 years. Sure <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="enough">anough</span> we are, that <i><span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="Britain">Britan</span></i> hath <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="been">bin</span> anciently <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="termed">term'd</span> <i>Albion</i>, both by the <i>Greeks</i> and <i>Romans</i>. And [<i>Mela</i> the Geographer](note:melageo) makes mention of a <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="stony">stonie</span> <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="shore">shoar</span> in [<i>Languedoc</i>](note:languedoc), where by report such a <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="Battle">Battel</span> was fought. The rest, as his giving name to the <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="Isle">Ile</span>, or ever landing <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="here">heer</span>, depends altogether upon late surmises. But too absurd, and too unconscionably gross is that fond invention that wafted hither the [fifty daughters](note:50daughters) of a strange <i>Dioclesian King of Syria</i>; brought in <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="doubtless">doubtles</span> by <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="some">som</span> <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="illiterate">illiterat</span> pretender to <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="something">somthing</span> <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="mistaken">mistak'n</span> in the Common Poetical Story of <i>Danaus King of Argos</i>, while his vanity, not <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="pleased">pleas'd</span> with the obscure beginning which truest Antiquity affords the Nation, <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="laboured">labour'd</span> to contrive us a Pedigree, as he thought, more noble. These Daughters by appointment of <i>Danaus</i> on the marriage-night having <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="murdered">murder'd</span> all <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="their">thir</span> Husbands, except [<i>Linceus</i>](note:lynceus), whom his <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="Wife&#x27;s">Wives</span> <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="loyalty">loialty</span> <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="saved">sav'd</span>, were by him at the suit of his Wife <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="their">thir</span> Sister, not put to death, but <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="turned">turn'd</span> out to Sea in a Ship <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="unmanned">unmann'd</span>; of which whole Sex they had <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="incurred">incurr'd</span> the hate: and as the Tale goes, were <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="driven">driv'n</span> on <i>this <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="Island">Iland</span></i>. Where the Inhabitants, none but Devils, as <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="some">som</span> write, or as others, a lawless crew left <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="here">heer</span> by <i>Albion</i>, without Head or <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="Governor">Governour</span>, both <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="entertained">entertain'd</span> them, and had issue by them a second Breed of Giants, who <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="tyrranized">tyranniz'd</span> <i>the <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="Isle">Ile</span></i>, till <i>Brutus</i> came.

The Eldest of these Dames in <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="their">thir</span> Legend they call <i>Albina</i>; and from thence, for which cause the whole scene was <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="framed">fram'd</span>, will have the name <i>Albion</i> deriv'd. Incredible it may seem so sluggish a conceit should prove so ancient, as to be <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="authorized">authoriz'd</span> by the Elder <i><span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="Nennius">[Ninnius](note:nennius)</span></i>, reputed to have <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="lived">liv'd</span> above a thousand years <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="ago">agoe</span>. This I [find not in him](note:holinshed); but that <i>Histion</i> sprung of <i>Japhet</i>, had four Sons; <i>Francus</i>, <i>Romanus</i>, <i>Alemannus</i>, and <i>Britto</i>, of whom the <i><span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="Britons">Britans</span></i>; as true, I <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="believe">beleeve</span>, as that those other Nations whose names are <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="resembled">resembl'd</span>, came of the other three; if these Dreams give not just occasion to call in doubt the Book it self, which bears that title.

Hitherto the things <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="themselves">themselvs</span> have <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="given">giv'n</span> us a warrantable dispatch to run them soon over. But now of <i>[Brutus](note:brutusline)</i> and his Line, with the whole Progeny of Kings, to the entrance of <i>Julius <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="Caesar">Cæsar</span></i>, we cannot so easily be <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="discharged">discharg'd</span>; Descents of Ancestry, long <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="continued">continu'd</span>, laws and exploits not plainly seeming to be <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="borrowed">borrow'd</span>, or <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="devised">devis'd</span>, which on the common <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="belief">beleif</span> have wrought no small impression: defended by many, <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="denied">deny'd</span> utterly by few. For what though <i>Brutus</i>, and the whole <i>Trojan</i> pretence were <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="yielded">yeelded</span> up, seeing they who first <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="devised">devis'd</span> to bring us from <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="some">som</span> noble Ancestor were content at first with <i>[Brutus](note:brutusconsul)</i> the Consul; till better invention, although not willing to <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="forgo">forgoe</span> the name, taught them to remove it higher into a more fabulous Age, and by the same remove lighting on the <i>Trojan</i> Tales in affectation to make the <i><span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="Briton">Britan</span></i> of one Original with the <i>Roman</i>, <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="pitched">pitch'd</span> there; yet those old and inborn names of successive Kings, never any to have <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="been">bin</span> real persons, or done in <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="their">thir</span> lives at least <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="some">som</span> part of what so long hath <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="been">bin</span> <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="remembered">remember'd</span>, cannot be thought without too strict an incredulity.

For these, and those causes above <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="mentioned">mention'd</span>, that which hath <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="received">receav'd</span> approbation from so many, I have <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="chosen">chos'n</span> not to <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="omit">omitt</span>. Certain or uncertain, be that upon the credit of those whom I must follow; so far as keeps <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="aloof">alooff</span> from impossible and absurd, attested by ancient Writers from Books more ancient I refuse not, as the due and proper subject of Story. The principal Author is well <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="known">know'n</span> to be <i>[Geoffrey of Monmouth](note:gmonmouth)</i>; what he was, and whence his authority, who in his Age, or before him, have <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="delivered">deliver'd</span> the same matter, and such like general discourses, will better stand in a Treatise by <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="themselves">themselvs</span>. [All of them](note:huntingdon1) agree in this, that <i>Brutus</i> was the Son of <i>Silvius</i>; he of <i>Ascanius</i>; whose Father was <i><span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="Aeneas">Æneas</span> a Trojan Prince</i>, who at the burning of that City, with his Son <i>Ascanius</i>, and a collected number that <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="escaped">escap'd</span>, after long <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="wandering">wandring</span> on the Sea, <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="arrived">arriv'd</span> in <i>Italy</i>. The prediction <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="failed">fail'd</span> not, for in <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="travail">travel</span> the Mother <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="died">di'd</span>. And Brutus (the Child was so <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="called">call'd</span>) at fifteen years of Age, attending his Father to the <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="Chase">Chace</span>, with an arrow unfortunately <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="killed">kill'd</span> him.

<span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="Banished">Banish'd</span> therfore by his kindred, he retires into <i>Greece</i>. Where meeting with the race of [<i>Helenus King <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="Priam&#x27;s">Priams</span> Son</i>](note:helenus), held there in servile condition by <i>Pandrasus then King</i>, with them he abides. For [<i>Pirrhus</i>](note:pirrhus) in revenge of his Father slain at <i>Troy</i>, had brought thither with him <i>Helenus</i>, and many others into servitude. There <i>Brutus</i> among his own stock so thrives in <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="virtue">vertue</span> and in Arms, as renders him <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="beloved">belov'd</span> to Kings, and great Captains above all the Youth of that Land. <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="Whereby">Wherby</span> the <i>Trojans</i> not only <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="begin">beginn</span> to hope, but secretly to move him, that he would lead them the way to liberty. They allege their numbers, and the <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="promised">promis'd</span> help of <i>Assaracus</i> a Noble <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="Greek">Greekish</span> Youth, by the <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="Mother&#x27;s">Mothers</span> side a <i>Trojan</i>; whom for that cause his Brother went about to dispossess of certain Castles <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="bequeathed">bequeath'd</span> him by his Father. <i>Brutus</i> considering both the Forces <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="offered">offer'd</span> him, and the strength of those Holds, not unwillingly consents.

As this may pass with good allowance, that the <i>Trojans</i> might be many in these parts, for <i>Helenus</i> was by <i>Pirrhus</i> made King of the <i>Chaonians</i>, and the Sons of <i>Pirrhus</i> by <i>Andromache <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="Hector&#x27;s">Hectors</span> Wife</i> could not but be powerful through all <i>Epirus</i>, so much the more it may be doubted, how these <i>Trojans</i> could be thus in bondage, where they had <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="Friends">Freinds</span> and <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="Countrymen">Country-men</span> so Potent.
