### <i>Edward</i> the Elder.

</div>

[<i>EDward</i>](note:edwardelder) the Son of <i>Alfred</i> succeeded, in [learning not equal](note:learningnot), in power and extent of Dominion, surpassing his Father. The beginning of his Reign had much disturbance by <i>Ethelwald</i> an ambitious young man, Son of the <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="King&#x27;s">Kings</span> Uncle, or <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="Cousin">Cosin</span> German, [or Brother](note:thebeginningof), for his Genealogy is variously <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="delivered">deliverd</span>. He vainly avouching to have [equal right](note:901) with <i>Edward</i> of [succession](note:successioncrown) to the Crown, <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="possessed">possess'd</span> himself of [<i><span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="Winburn">Winburne</span></i>](note:winburne) in <i>Dorset</i>, and another Town <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="diversely">diversly</span> <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="named">nam'd</span>, giving out that there he would live or <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="die">dye</span>; but <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="encompassed">encompass'd</span> with the <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="King&#x27;s">Kings</span> Forces at <i><span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="Badbury">Badburie</span></i> a place nigh, his heart failing him, he stole out by night, and fled to the <i>Danish</i> Army beyond <i>Humber</i>. The King sent after him, but not overtaking, found his Wife in the Town, whom he had married out of a Nunnery, and commanded her to be [sent back](note:902) <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="thither">thether</span>. About this time the <i>Kentish</i> men, against a multitude of <i>Danish</i> <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="Pirates">Pirats</span>, fought prosperously at a place <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="called">call'd</span> <i>Holme</i>, as [<i>Hoveden</i> records](note:hovedenrecords). <i>Ethelwald</i> aided by the <i>Northumbrians</i> with Shipping, [three years after](note:905), sailing to the <i>East-Angles</i>, <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="persuaded">perswaded</span> the <i>Danes</i> there [to fall into](note:tofallinto) the <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="King&#x27;s">Kings</span> Territory, who marching with him as far as <i><span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="Cricklade">Crecklad</span></i>, and passing the <i>Thames</i> there, wasted as far beyond as they durst venture, and <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="laden">lad'n</span> with spoils <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="returned">return'd</span> home. The King with his Powers <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="making">makeing</span> speed after them, between the <i>Dike</i> and <i>Ouse</i>, <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="supposed">suppos'd</span> to be <i>Suffolk</i> and <i>Cambridge-shire</i>, as far as the Fenns Northward, laid <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="waste">wast</span> all before him. Thence intending to return, he commanded that all his Army should follow him close without delay; but the <i>Kentish</i> men, though <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="often">oft'n</span> <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="called">call'd</span> upon, lagging behind, the <i>Danish</i> Army prevented them, and <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="joined">join'd</span> <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="Battle">Battel</span> with the King: where Duke <i>Sigulf</i> and Earl <i>Sigelm</i>, with many other of the Nobles were slain; on the <i><span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="Danes&#x27;">Danes</span></i> part, <i>Eoric</i> <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="their">thir</span> King, and <i>Ethelwald</i> the Author of this War, with others of high note, and of them greater number, but with great ruin on both sides; yet the <i>Danes</i> kept in <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="their">thir</span> power the burying of <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="their">thir</span> slain. What ever <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="followed">follow'd</span> upon this conflict, which we read not, the King [two years after](note:907) with the <i>Danes</i>, both of <i>East-Angles</i> and <i>Northumberland</i>, [concluded peace](note:concludedpeace), which <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="continued">continu'd</span> [three years](note:910), by whomsoever <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="broken">brok'n</span>: for at the end thereof King <i>Edward</i> raising [great Forces](note:greatforcesedward) out of <i><span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="Wessex">West-Sex</span></i>, and <i>Mercia</i>, sent them against the <i>Danes</i> beyond <i>Humber</i>; where staying five weeks, they made great spoil and slaughter. The King <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="offered">offer'd</span> them terms of peace, but they rejecting all, <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="entered">enterd</span> with the [next year](note:911) into <i>Mercia</i>, <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="rendering">rendring</span> [no less hostility](note:nolesshostility) <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="than">then</span> they had <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="suffered">suffer'd</span>; but at <i>Tetnal</i> in <i>Staffordshire</i>, saith <i>Florent</i>, were by the English in a set <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="Battle">Battel</span> overthrown. King <i>Edward</i> then in <i>Kent</i>, had got together of Ships about an <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="hundred">hunderd</span> Sail, others <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="gone">gon</span> Southward, came back and met him. The <i>Danes</i> now supposing that his main Forces were upon the Sea, took liberty to rove and plunder up and down, as [hope of prey](note:hopeofprey) led them, beyond <i>Severn</i>. The King guessing what might <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="embolden">imbold'n</span> them, sent before him the lightest of his Army to entertain them; Then following with the rest, set upon them in <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="their">thir</span> return over <i><span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="Cambridge">Cantbrig</span></i> in <i><span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="Glocestershire">Glostershire</span></i>, and slew many thousands, among whom <i>Ecwils</i>, <i>Hafden</i>, and <i>Hinguar</i> <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="their">thir</span> Kings, and many other harsh names in <i>Huntingdon</i>; the place also of this fight is variously <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="written">writt'n</span> by <i>Ethelwerd</i> and <i>Florent</i>, <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="called">call'd</span> <i><span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="Wodensfield">Wodensfeild</span></i>. The [year following](note:912) <i>Ethred</i> Duke of <i>Mercia</i>, to whom <i>Alfred</i> had <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="given">giv'n</span> <i>London</i>, with [his daughter](note:hisdaughter) in marriage, now dying, King <i>Edward</i> <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="resumed">resum'd</span> that City, and <i>Oxford</i>, with the Countries adjoining, into his own hands, and the [year after](note:913), built, or much <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="repaired">repair'd</span> by his <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="Soldiers">Souldiers</span>, [the Town](note:thetown) of <i>Hertford</i> on either side <i>Lee</i>, and leaving a sufficient number at the work, <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="marched">march'd</span> about middle Summer, with the other part of his Forces into <i>Essex</i>, and <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="encamped">encamp'd</span> at <i>Maldon</i>, while his <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="Soldiers">Souldiers</span> built <i>Witham</i>; where a good part of the Country, subject formerly to the <i>Danes</i>, <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="yielded">yeilded</span> themselves to his protection. <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="Four">Fowr</span> [years after](note:917) (<i>Florent</i> [allows but one](note:allowsbutone) year) the <i>Danes</i> from <i><span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="Leicester">Leister</span></i> and <i>Northampton</i>, falling into <i>Oxfordshire</i>, committed much rapine, and in some Towns <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="thereof">therof</span> great slaughter; while another party wasting <i>Hertfordshire</i>, met with other Fortune: for the Country-people <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="inured">inur'd</span> now to such kind of incursions, joining stoutly together, fell upon the spoilers, and <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="recovered">recover'd</span> <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="their">thir</span> own goods, with some booty from <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="their">thir</span> Enemies. About the same time <i>Elfled</i> the <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="King&#x27;s">Kings</span> Sister sent her Army of <i>Mercians</i> into <i>Wales</i>, who routed the <i><span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="Welsh">Welch</span></i>, [took the Castle](note:tookthecastle) of <i>Bricnam-mere</i> by <i>Brecknock</i>, and brought away the <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="King&#x27;s">Kings</span> Wife of that Country with other <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="Prisoners">Prisners</span>. Not long after she took <i>Derby</i> from the <i>Danes</i>, and the Castle by a sharp assault. But [the year](note:918) <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="ensuing">ensueing</span> brought [a new Fleet](note:anewfleetdanes) of <i>Danes</i> to <i>Lidwic</i> in <i>Devonshire</i>, under two Leaders, <i>Otter</i> and <i>Roald</i>; who sailing thence Westward about the <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="land&#x27;s">lands</span> end, came up to the mouth of <i>Severn</i>; there landing wasted the <i><span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="Welsh">Welch</span></i> Coast, and <i><span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="Irchenfield">Irchenfeild</span></i> part of <i>Herefordshire</i>; where they took [<i>Kuneleac</i>](note:kuneleac) a <i>British</i> Bishop, for whose <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="ransom">ransome</span> King <i>Edward</i> gave forty pound: but the men of <i>Hereford</i> and <i><span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="Glocestershire">Glostershire</span></i> assembling, put them to flight; slaying <i>Roald</i> and the Brother of <i>Otter</i>, with many more, <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="pursued">persu'd</span> them to a Wood, and there beset, <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="compelled">compel'd</span> them to give hostages of present departure. The King with his Army sat not far off, securing from the South of <i>Severn</i> to <i>Avon</i>; so that <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="openly">op'nly</span> they durst not, by night they twice <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="ventured">ventur'd</span> to land; but found such welcome, that few of them came back; the rest <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="anchored">anchord</span> by a small <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="Island">Iland</span> where many of them <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="famished">famish'd</span>; then sailing to a place <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="called">call'd</span> <i>Deomed</i>, they <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="crossed">cross'd</span> into <i>Ireland</i>. The King with his Army went to <i>Buckingham</i>, <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="stayed">staid</span> there a <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="month">moneth</span>, and built two Castles or Forts on either Bank of <i>Ouse</i> ere his departing, and <i>Turkitel</i> a <i>Danish</i> Leader, with those of <i>Bedford</i> and <i>Northampton</i>, <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="yielded">yeilded</span> him subjection. <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="Whereupon">Wherupon</span> the [next year](note:919) he came [with his Army](note:withhisarmy) to the Town of <i>Bedford</i>, took possession <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="thereof">therof</span>, <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="stayed">staid</span> there a month, and gave order to build another part of the Town, on the South-side of <i>Ouse</i>. Thence [the year](note:920) following went again to <i>Maldon</i>, <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="repaired">repair'd</span> and [<span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="fortified">fortifi'd</span>](note:fortified) the Town. <i>Turkitel</i> the <i>Dane</i> having small hope to thrive <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="here">heer</span>, where things with such prudence were <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="managed">mannag'd</span> against his <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="interest">interess</span>, got leave of the King, with as many voluntaries as would follow him, to pass into <i>France</i>. Early the [next year](note:921) King <i>Edward</i> <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="re-edified">re-edifi'd</span> <i>Tovechester</i>, now <i>Torchester</i>; and [another City](note:anothercity) in the Annals <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="called">call'd</span> <i>Wigingmere</i>. Mean while the <i>Danes</i> of <i><span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="Leicester">Leister</span></i> and <i>Northampton-shire</i>, not <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="liking">likeing</span> perhaps to be <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="neighbored">neighbour'd</span> with Strong Towns, laid <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="Siege">Seige</span> to <i>Torchester</i>; \[but they within repelling the assault one whole day till supplies came\] quitted the <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="Siege">Seige</span> by night; and <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="pursued">persu'd</span> close by the <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="besieged">beseig'd</span>, between <i>Birnwud</i> and <i>Ailsbury</i> were <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="surprised">surpris'd</span>, many of them made <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="Prisoners">Prisners</span>, and much of <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="their">thir</span> baggage lost. Other of the <i>Danes</i> at <i>Huntingdon</i>, aided from the <i>East-Angles</i>, finding that Castle not commodious, left it, and built another at <i>Temsford</i>, judging that place more opportune from whence to make <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="their">thir</span> excursions; and soon after went forth with design to assail <i>Bedford</i>: but the Garrison issuing out, slew a great part of them, the rest fled. After this a greater Army of them <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="gathered">gatherd</span> out of <i>Mercia</i> and the <i>East-Angles</i>, came and <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="besieged">beseig'd</span> the City <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="called">call'd</span> <i>Wigingmere</i> a whole Day; but finding it defended stoutly by them within, thence also departed, driving away much of <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="their">thir</span> <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="Cattle">Cattel</span>: wherupon the English from Towns and <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="Cities">Citties</span> round about joining Forces, laid <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="Siege">Seige</span> to the Town and Castle of <i>Temsford</i>, and by assault took both; slew <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="their">thir</span> King with <i>Toglea</i> a Duke, and <i>Mannan</i> his Son an Earl, with all the rest there found; who chose to die rather <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="than">then</span> <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="yield">yeild</span>. <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="Encouraged">Encourag'd</span> by this, the men of <i>Kent</i>, <i>Surrey</i>, and part of <i>Essex</i>, enterprise the <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="Siege">Seige</span> of <i>Colchester</i>, nor gave over till they won it, sacking the Town and putting to Sword all the <i>Danes</i> therein, except some who <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="escaped">escap'd</span> over the Wall. To the <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="succor">succour</span> of these, a great number of <i>Danes</i> inhabiting Ports and other Towns in the <i>East-Angles</i>, united <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="their">thir</span> Force; but coming too late, as in revenge <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="beleagered">beleaguerd</span> <i>Maldon</i>; but that Town also timely <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="relieved">releiv'd</span>, they departed, not only frustrate of <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="their">thir</span> design, but so hotly <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="pursued">persu'd</span>, that many thousands of them lost <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="their">thir</span> lives in the flight. The King also of a people thereabout <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="called">call'd</span> <i>Streatgledwalli</i>, (the <i>North <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="Welsh">Welch</span></i>, as <i>Camden</i> thinks, of <i><span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="Strathclyde">Strat-Cluid</span></i> in <i>Denbigh-shire</i>, perhaps rather the <i>British</i> of <i>Cumberland</i> ) did him homage, and not <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="undeserved">undeserv'd</span>.
