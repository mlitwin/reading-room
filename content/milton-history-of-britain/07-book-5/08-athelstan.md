### <i>Athelstan</i>.

</div>

[<i>AThelstan</i>](note:athelstan) next in age to <i>Ethelward</i> his Brother, who <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="deceased">deceas'd</span> untimely few days before, though born of a Concubine, yet for the great appearance of many <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="virtues">vertues</span> in him, and his Brethren being yet under Age, [was exalted](note:926) to the Throne at <i><span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="Kingston">Kingstone</span></i> upon <i>Thames</i>, and by his <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="Father&#x27;s">Fathers</span> last Will, [saith <i><span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="Malmesbury">Malmsbury</span></i>](note:saithmalms), yet not without some opposition of one <i><span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="Elfred">Alfred</span></i> and his Accomplices; who not <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="liking">likeing</span> he should reign, had <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="conspired">conspir'd</span> to <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="seize">seise</span> on him after his <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="Father&#x27;s">Fathers</span> Death, and to put out his Eyes. But the <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="Conspirators">Conspiratours</span> <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="discovered">discoverd</span>, and <i><span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="Elfred">Alfred</span></i> denying the Plot, was sent to <i>Rome</i>, to assert [his innocence](note:hisinnocence) before the Pope; where taking his Oath on the Altar, he fell down <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="immediately">immediatly</span>, and carried out by his Servants, three <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="days">daies</span> after <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="died">dy'd</span>. Mean while beyond <i>Humber</i>, the <i>Danes</i>, though much <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="awed">aw'd</span>, were not idle. <i>Inguald</i>, one of <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="their">thir</span> Kings, [took possession](note:tookpossession) of <i><span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="York">Yorke</span></i>, <i>Sitric</i>, who some years before had slain <i>Niel</i> his Brother, by force took <i>Davenport</i> in <i>Cheshire</i>; and however he defended these doings; grew so considerable, that <i>Athelstan</i> with great solemnity [gave him his Sister](note:gavesister) <i><span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="Eadgith">Edgith</span></i> to wife: but he <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="enjoyed">enjoy'd</span> her not long, dying ere the <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="year&#x27;s">years</span> end, nor his Sons <i>Anlaf</i> and <i>Guthfert</i> the <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="Kingdom">Kingdome</span>, <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="driven">driv'n</span> out the [next year](note:927) by <i>Athelstan</i>; [not unjustly](note:notunjustly) saith <i>Huntingdon</i>, as being first raisers of the War. <i>Simeon</i> calls him [<i>Gudfrid</i>](note:gudfrid) a <i>British</i> King, whom <i>Athelstan</i> this year drove out of his <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="Kingdom">Kingdome</span>; and perhaps they were both one, the name and time not much differing, the place only <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="mistaken">mistak'n</span>. <i><span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="Malmesbury">Malmsbury</span></i> [differs in the name also](note:differsinthename), calling him <i>Aldulf</i> a certain Rebel. Them also I wish as much <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="mistaken">mistak'n</span>, who write that <i>Athelstan</i>, jealous of his younger Brother <i>Edwin's</i> towardly <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="virtues">vertues</span>, <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="least">lest</span> added to the right of Birth, they might some time or other [call in question](note:933) his [illegitimate](note:illegitimate) precedence, <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="caused">caus'd</span> him to be <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="drowned">drown'd</span> in the Sea; <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="exposed">expos'd</span>, some say, with one Servant in a <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="rotten">rott'n</span> Bark, without Sail or Oar; where the youth far off land, and in rough weather despairing, threw himself over-board; the Servant more patient, got to land and reported the success. But this <i><span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="Malmesbury">Malmsbury</span></i> confesses to be sung in old Songs, not read in warrantable Authors: and <i>Huntingdon</i> speaks as of a sad accident to <i>Athelstan</i>, that he lost his Brother <i>Edwin</i> by Sea; far the more credible, in that <i>Athelstan</i>, as it is <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="written">writ'n</span> by all, tenderly <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="loved">lov'd</span> and bred up the rest of his Brethren, of whom he had no less cause to be jealous. And the [year following](note:934) he <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="propsered">prosperd</span> better <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="than">then</span> from so foul a fact, passing into <i>Scotland</i> with [great puissance](note:greatpuissance), both by Sea and Land, and <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="chasing">chaceing</span> his Enemies before him, by Land as far as [<i>Dunfeoder</i>](note:dunfeoder), and <i>Wertermore</i>, by Sea as far as <i>Cathness</i>. The [cause of this expedition](note:causeofthis), saith <i><span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="Malmesbury">Malmsbury</span></i>, was to demand <i>Gudfert</i> the Son of <i>Sitric</i>, <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="thither">thether</span> fled, though not <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="denied">deny'd</span> at length by <i>Constantine</i>, who with <i>Eugenius</i> King of <i>Cumberland</i>, at a place <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="called">call'd</span> <i>Dacor</i> or <i>Dacre</i> in that Shire, <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="surrendered">surrenderd</span> himself and each his <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="Kingdom">Kingdome</span> to <i>Athelstan</i>, who brought back with him [for hostage](note:forhostage) the Son of <i>Constantine</i>. But <i>Gudfert</i> escaping in the mean while out of <i>Scotland</i>, and <i>Constantine</i> exasperated by this invasion, <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="persuaded">perswaded</span> <i>Anlaf</i> the other Son of <i>Sitric</i> then fled into <i>Ireland</i>, others write <i>Anlaf</i> King of <i>Ireland</i> and the <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="Isles">Iles</span>, his Son in law, with [615 Ships](note:615ships), and the King of <i>Cumberland</i> with [other forces](note:938), to his aid. This within <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="four">fowr</span> years effected, [they <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="entered">enterd</span>](note:theyenterd) <i>England</i> by <i>Humber</i>, and fought with <i>Athelstan</i> at a place <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="called">call'd</span> <i>Wendune</i>, others term it <i>Brunanburg</i>, others <i>Bruneford</i>, which <i>Ingulf</i> places beyond <i>Humber</i>, <i>Camden</i> in <i>Glendale</i> of <i>Northumberland</i> on the <i>Scotch</i> Borders; the bloodiest fight, say Authors, that ever this <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="Island">Iland</span> saw, to describe which the <i>Saxon</i> Annalist wont to be sober and succinct, whether the same or another writer, now <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="laboring">labouring</span> under the weight of his Argument, and <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="over-charged">over-charg'd</span>, runs on a sudden into such [extravagant <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="fancies">fansies</span> and metaphors](note:battlepoem), as bare him quite beside the scope of being understood. <i>Huntingdon</i>, though himself peccant enough in this kind, transcribes him [word for word](note:wordforword) as a pastime to his Readers. I shall only <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="sum">summe</span> up what of him I can attain, in <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="usual">usuall</span> language. The <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="Battle">Battel</span> was fought eagerly from morning till night; some fell of King <i><span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="Edward&#x27;s">Edwards</span></i> [old Army](note:oldarmy), <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="tried">try'd</span> in many a <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="Battle">Battel</span> before; but on the other side great multitudes, the rest fled to <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="their">thir</span> Ships. Five Kings, and 7 of <i>Anlaf's</i> Chief Captains were slain on the place, with [<i>Froda</i>](note:froda), a <i>Norman</i> Leader; <i>Constantine</i> <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="escaped">escap'd</span> home, but lost his Son in the fight, if I understand [my Author](note:myauthorwheeloc); <i>Anlaf</i> by Sea to <i>Dublin</i>, with a small remainder of his great <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="host">hoast</span>. <i><span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="Malmesbury">Malmsbury</span></i> [relates this War](note:relatesthiswar), adding many circumstances after this manner: That <i>Anlaf</i> joining with <i>Constantine</i> and the whole power of <i>Scotland</i>, besides those which he brought with him out of <i>Ireland</i>, came on far Southwards, till <i>Athelstan</i> who had <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="retired">retir'd</span> on set purpose to be the surer of his <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="Enemies">Enimies</span>, <span numerous, <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="resolved">resolv'd</span> first to <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="spy">spie</span> in what posture his Enemies lay: and imitating perhaps what he heard attempted by King <i>Alfred</i> the Age before, in the habit of a <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="Musician">Musitian</span>, got access by his lute and voice to the <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="King&#x27;s">Kings</span> tent, there playing both the minstrel and the <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="spy">spie</span>: then towards Evening <span data-gloss="been">bin</span> his <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="Soldier">Souldier</span> and well knew him, <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="viewing">veiwing</span> earnestly the <span The <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="Soldier">Souldier</span> forthwith acquaints the King, and by him <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="blamed">blam'd</span> for letting go his Enemy, <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="answered">answerd</span>, that he had <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="given">giv'n</span> first his military Oath to <i>Anlaf</i>, whom if he had <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="betrayed">betrai'd</span>, the King might suspect him of like treasonous <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="mind">minde</span> towards himself; which to disprove, he <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="advised">advis'd</span> him to remove his Tent a good distance off; and so done, it <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="happened">happ'nd</span> that a Bishop with his retinue coming that night to the Army, <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="pitched">pich'd</span> his Tent in the same place from whence the King had <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="removed">remov'd</span>. <i>Anlaf</i> coming by night as he had <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="designed">design'd</span>, to assault the Camp and especially the <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="King&#x27;s">Kings</span> Tent, finding there the Bishop in stead, slew him with all his followers. <i>Athelstan</i> took the <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="Alarm">Allarm</span>, and as it seems, was not found so unprovided, but that the day now appearing, he put his men in order, and <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="maintained">maintain'd</span> the fight till Evening; <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="wherein">wherin</span> <i>Constantine</i> himself was slain with five other Kings, and twelve Earls, the Annals were content with <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="seven">seav'n</span>, in the rest [not disagreeing](note:notdisagreeing). <i>Ingulf</i> Abbot of <i>Croyland</i> from the <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="authority">autority</span> of <i>Turketul</i> a principal Leader in this <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="Battle">Battel</span>, relates it more at large to [this effect](note:thiseffectingulf): that <i>Athelstan</i> above a mile distant from the place where execution was done upon the Bishop and his supplies, allarm'd at the noise, came down by break of day upon <i>Anlaf</i> and his Army, <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="overwatched">overwatch't</span> and wearied now with the slaughter they had made, and something out of order, yet in two main <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="Battles">Battels</span>. The King <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="therefore">therfore</span> in like manner dividing, led the one part consisting most of <i>West Saxons</i>, against <i>Anlaf</i> with his <i>Danes</i> and <i>Irish</i>, committing the other to his Chancellor <i>Turketul</i>, with the <i>Mercians</i> and <i>Londoners</i>, against <i>Constantine</i> and his <i>Scots</i>. The <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="shower">showr</span> of Arrows and Darts <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="over-passed">over-pass't</span>, both <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="Battles">Battells</span> <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="attacked">attack'd</span> each other with a close and terrible <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="engagement">ingagement</span>, for a long space neither side giving ground. Till the Chancellor <i>Turketul</i>, a man of great stature and strength, taking with him a few <i>Londoners</i> of select <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="valor">valour</span>, and <i>Singin</i> who led the <i><span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="Worcestershire">Worstershire</span></i> men, a Captain of undaunted courage, broke into the thickest, making his way first through the <i>Picts</i> and <i>Orkeners</i>, then through the <i>Cumbrians</i> and <i>Scots</i>, and came at length where <i>Constantine</i> himself fought, <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="unhorsed">unhors'd</span> him, and <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="used">us'd</span> all means to take him alive; but the <i>Scots</i> valiantly defending <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="their">thir</span> King, and laying load upon <i>Turketul</i>, which the goodness of his <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="Armor">Armour</span> well <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="endured">endur'd</span>, he had yet <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="been">bin</span> <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="beaten">beat'n</span> down, had not <i>Singin</i> his <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="faithful">faithfull</span> second at the same time slain <i>Constantine</i>; which once known, <i>Anlaf</i> and the whole Army betook them to flight, <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="whereof">wherof</span> a huge multitude fell by the Sword. This <i>Turketul</i> not long after leaving worldly Affairs, became Abbot of <i>Croyland</i>, which at his own cost he had <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="repaired">repair'd</span>, from <i>Danish</i> ruins, and left there this memorial of his former actions. <i>Athelstan</i> with his Brother <i>Edmund</i> victorious, thence turning into <i>Wales</i>, with much more ease <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="vanquished">vanquish'd</span> <i>Ludwal</i> the King, and <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="possessed">possesst</span> his land. But <i><span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="Malmesbury">Malmsbury</span></i> writes, that commiserating human chance, as he <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="displaced">displac'd</span>, so he <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="restored">restor'd</span> both him and <i>Constantine</i> to <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="their">thir</span> Regal State; for the surrender of King <i>Constantine</i> hath <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="been">bin</span> above <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="spoken">spok'n</span> of. However the <i><span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="Welsh">Welch</span></i> did him homage at the City of <i>Hereford</i>, and <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="covenanted">covnanted</span> yearly payment of Gold 20 pound, of Silver 300, of <i>Oxen</i> 25 thousand, besides Hunting Dogs and Hawks. He also took <i>Exeter</i> from the <i>Cornish</i> <i><span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="Britons">Britans</span></i>, who till that time had equal right there with the English, and bounded them with the River <i>Tamar</i>, as the other <i><span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="British">Brittish</span></i> with <i>Wey</i>. Which words the King laying to heart, and <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="pondering">pondring</span> how ill he had done to make away his Brother, <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="avenged">aveng'd</span> himself first on the adviser of that fact, took on him <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="seven">seav'n</span> <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="year&#x27;s">years</span> penance, and as <i><span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="Matthew">Mat.</span> <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="Westminster">West.</span></i> saith, [built two Monasteries](note:twomonasteries) for the Soul of his Brother. His Laws are extant among the Laws of other <i>Saxon</i> Kings to this day.
