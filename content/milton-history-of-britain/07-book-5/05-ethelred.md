### <i>Ethelred</i>.

</div>

<i>Ethelred</i> the third, Son of <i>Ethelwolf</i>, at his [first coming](note:firstcoming) to the Crown was <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="entertained">entertain'd</span> with a [fresh invasion](note:freshinvasion) of <i>Danes</i>, led by <i>Hinguar</i> and <i>Hubba</i>, two Brothers, who now had got footing among the <i>East-Angles</i>; there they <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="wintered">winterd</span>, and coming to terms of peace with the Inhabitants, <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="furnished">furnish'd</span> themselves of Horses, forming by that means many Troops with Riders of <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="their">thir</span> own: These [Pagans](note:thesepagans), <i>Asser</i> saith, came from the River <i>Danubius</i>. Fitted thus for a long expedition, they <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="ventured">ventur'd</span> the [next year](note:next2year) to make <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="their">thir</span> way [over land](note:overland) and over <i>Humber</i>, as far as <i><span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="York">Yorke</span></i>, them they found to <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="their">thir</span> hands <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="embrolied">imbroil'd</span> in civil dissensions; <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="their">thir</span> King <i>Osbert</i> they had thrown out, and <i>Ella</i> Leader of another faction chosen in his room; who both, though late, <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="admonished">admonish'd</span> by <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="their">thir</span> common danger, towards the <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="year&#x27;s">years</span> end with united powers made head against the <i>Danes</i> and <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="prevailed">prevail'd</span>; but <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="pursuing">persueing</span> them over-eagerly into <i><span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="York">Yorke</span></i>, then but [slenderly <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="walled">wall'd</span>](note:slenderly), the <i>Northumbrians</i> were every where <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="slaughtered">slaughter'd</span>, both within and without; <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="their">thir</span> Kings also both slain, <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="their">thir</span> City <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="burned">burnt</span>, saith <i><span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="Malmesbury">Malmsbury</span></i>, the rest as they could, made <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="their">thir</span> peace, over-run and <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="vanquished">vanquisht</span> as far as the River <i>Tine</i>, and <i>Egbert</i> of English race appointed King over them. <i>[Bromton](note:brompton)</i> no <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="ancient">antient</span> Author (for he wrote since <i><span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="Matthew">Mat.</span> <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="Westminster">West.</span></i> ) nor of much credit, writes a particular cause of the <i>Danes</i> coming to <i><span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="York">Yorke</span></i>: that <i>Bruern</i> a Nobleman, whose Wife King <i>Osbert</i> had <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="ravished">ravisht</span>, <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="called">call'd</span> in <i>Hinguar</i> and <i>Hubba</i> to revenge him. The example is remarkable, [if the truth](note:ifthetruth) were as evident. Thence victorious, the <i>Danes</i> next year <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="entered">enterd</span> into <i>Mercia</i> towards <i>Nottingham</i>, where they spent the Winter. <i>Burhed</i> then King of that Country, unable to resist, implores the Aid of <i>Ethelred</i> and young <i>Alfred</i> his Brother, they assembling <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="their">thir</span> Forces and joining with the <i>Mercians</i> about <i>Nottingham</i>, offer <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="Battle">Battel</span>: the <i>Danes</i> not daring to [come forth](note:comeforth), kept themselves within that Town and Castle, so that no great fight was hazarded there; at length the <i>Mercians</i> weary of long <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="suspense">suspence</span>, <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="entered">enterd</span> into conditions of peace with <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="their">thir</span> Enemies. After which the <i>Danes</i> returning back to <i><span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="York">Yorke</span></i>, made <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="their">thir</span> abode there the space of [one year](note:spaceyear), committing, some say, many cruelties. Thence <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="embarking">imbarking</span> to <i>Lindsey</i>, and [all the Summer](note:allsummer) destroying [that Country](note:thatcountry), about September they came with [like fury](note:likefury) into <i>Kesteven</i>, another part of <i>Lincolnshire</i>, where <i>Algar</i> the Earl of <i>Howland</i> now <i>Holland</i>, with his Forces, and two <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="hundred">hunderd</span> stout <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="Soldiers">Souldiers</span> belonging to the Abbey of <i>Croiland</i>, three <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="hundred">hunderd</span> from about <i>Boston</i>, <i>Morcard</i> Lord of <i>Brunne</i>, with his numerous Family, well <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="trained">train'd</span> and <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="armed">arm'd</span>: <i>Osgot</i> <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="Governor">Governour</span> of <i>Lincoln</i>, with 500. of that City, all <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="joining">joyning</span> together, gave <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="Battle">Battel</span> to the <i>Danes</i>, slew of them a great multitude, with three of <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="their">thir</span> Kings, and <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="pursued">persu'd</span> the rest to <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="their">thir</span> Tents; but the Night following <i>Gothrun</i>, <i>Baseg</i>, <i>Osketil</i>, <i>Halfden</i>, and <i>Hamond</i>, five Kings, and as many Earls, <i>Frena</i>, <i>Hinguar</i>, <i>Hubba</i>, <i>Sidroc</i> the Elder and Younger, coming in from several parts with great forces and spoils, great part of the English began to slink home. Nevertheless <i>Algar</i> with such as forsook him not, all next day in order of <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="Battle">Battel</span> facing the <i>Danes</i>, and sustaining <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="unmoved">unmov'd</span> the brunt of <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="their">thir</span> assaults, could not withhold his men at last from <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="pursuing">persueing</span> <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="their">thir</span> <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="counterfeited">counterfitted</span> fight; <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="whereby">wherby</span> <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="opened">op'nd</span> and <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="disordered">disorder'd</span>, they fell into the snare of <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="their">thir</span> Enemies, rushing back upon them. <i>Algar</i> and those Captains <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="fore-named">fore-nam'd</span> with him, all resolute men, retreating to a hill side, and slaying of such as <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="followed">follow'd</span> them, manifold <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="their">thir</span> own number, <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="died">dy'd</span> at length upon heaps of dead which they had made round about them. The <i>Danes</i> thence passing on into the Country of <i>East-Angles</i>, <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="rifled">rifl'd</span> and <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="burned">burnt</span> the Monastery of <i>Elie</i>, overthrew Earl <i>Wulketul</i> with his whole Army, and <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="lodged">lodg'd</span> out the Winter at <i>Thetford</i>; where King <i>Edmund</i> assailing them, was with his whole Army put to flight, himself <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="taken">tak'n</span>, bound to a stake, and shot to Death with Arrows, his whole Country <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="subdued">subdu'd</span>. The [next year](note:nextyear65) with [great supplies](note:greatsupplies), saith <i>Huntingdon</i>, bending <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="their">thir</span> march toward the <i>West-Saxons</i>, the only people now left in whom might seem yet to remain strength or courage likely to oppose them, they came to <i>Reading</i>, <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="fortified">fortifi'd</span> there between the two Rivers of <i>Thames</i>, and <i>Kenet</i>, and about three <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="days">dayes</span> after sent out wings of Horse under two Earls to [forage the Country](note:forage); but <i>Ethelwulf</i> Earl of <i><span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="Berkshire">Barkshire</span></i>, at <i>Englefield</i> a Village nigh, <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="encountered">encounterd</span> them, slew one of <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="their">thir</span> Earls, and <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="obtained">obtain'd</span> a great Victory. Four <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="days">dayes</span> after came the King himself and his Brother <i>Alfred</i> with the main <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="Battle">Battail</span>; and the <i>Danes</i> issuing forth, a bloody fight began, on either side great slaughter in which Earl <i>Ethelwulf</i> lost his life; but the <i>Danes</i> <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="losing">loosing</span> no ground, kept <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="their">thir</span> place of standing to the end. Neither did the English for this make less <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="haste">hast</span> to another conflict at <i>Escesdune</i> or <i>Ashdown</i>, four <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="days">dayes</span> after, where both Armies with <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="their">thir</span> whole force on either side met. The <i>Danes</i> were <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="embattled">imbattail'd</span> in two great Bodies, the one led by <i>Bascai</i> and <i>Halfden</i>, <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="their">thir</span> two Kings, the other by such Earls as were appointed; in like manner the English divided their powers, <i>Ethelred</i> the King stood against their Kings; and though on the lower ground, and coming later into the <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="Battle">Battail</span> from his Orisons, gave a fierce onset, wherein <i>Bascai</i> (the <i>Danish</i> [History](note:danishhistory) names him <i>Erazus</i> the Son of <i>Regicerus</i>) was slain. <i>Alfred</i> was <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="placed">plac'd</span> against the Earls, and beginning the <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="Battle">Battail</span> ere his Brother came into the field, with such resolution <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="charged">charg'd</span> them, that in the shock most of them were slain; they are named <i>Sidroc</i> Elder and Younger, <i>Osbern</i>, <i>Frean</i>, <i>Harald</i>; at length in both Divisions, the <i>Danes</i> turn <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="their">thir</span> backs; many thousands of them cut off, the rest <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="pursued">persu'd</span> till night. So much the more it may be <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="wondered">wonderd</span> to hear next in the Annals, that the <i>Danes</i> 14 days after such an overthrow, fighting again with <i>Ethelred</i> and his Brother <i>Alfred</i> at <i>Basing</i>, under conduct, saith the <i>Danish</i> History, of <i>Agnerus</i> and <i>Hubbo</i>, Brothers of the slain <i>Evacus</i>, should obtain the Victory; especially since the new supply of <i>Danes</i> <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="mentioned">mention'd</span> by <i>[Asser](note:mentioned)</i>, <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="arrived">arriv'd</span> after this action. But after two Months the King and his Brother fought with them again at <i>Mertun</i>, in two Squadrons as before, in which fight hard it is to understand who had the better; so darkly do the <i>Saxon Annals</i> deliver <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="their">thir</span> meaning with more <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="than">then</span> wonted infancy. Yet [these I take](note:theseitake) (for <i>Asser</i> is <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="here">heer</span> silent) to be the Chief Fountain of our story, the ground and basis upon which the Monks later in time gloss and comment at <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="their">thir</span> pleasure. Nevertheless it appears, that on the <i>Saxon</i> part, not <i>Heamund</i> the Bishop only, but many valiant men lost <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="their">thir</span> lives. This fight was <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="followed">follow'd</span> by a heavy Summer Plague; <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="whereof">wherof</span>, [as is thought](note:asisthought), King <i>Ethelred</i> <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="died">dy'd</span> in the fifth of his Reign, and was buried at <i>Winburne</i>, where his Epitaph inscribes that he had his <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="Death&#x27;s">Deaths</span> wound by the <i>Danes</i>, according to the <i>Danish</i> History 872. Of all these terrible landings and devastations by the <i>Danes</i>, from the days of <i>Ethelwolf</i> till <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="their">thir</span> two last <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="Battles">Battels</span> with <i>Ethelred</i>, or of <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="their">thir</span> Leaders, whether Kings, Dukes, or Earls, the <i>Danish</i> History of best credit saith nothing; So little Wit or Conscience it seems they had to leave any memory of <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="their">thir</span> brutish rather <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="than">then</span> manly actions; unless we shall suppose them to have come, as above was cited out of <i>Asser</i>, from <i>Danubius</i>, rather <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="than">then</span> from <i><span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="Denmark">Denmarke</span></i>, more probable some barbarous Nations of <i>Prussia</i>, or <i>Livonia</i>, not long before seated more Northward on the <i>Baltic</i> Sea.
