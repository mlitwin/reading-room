### <i>Edwi</i>.

</div>

[<i>EDwi</i>](note:edwigking) the Son of <i>Edmund</i> now [come to Age](note:cometoage), after his Uncle <i>Edred's</i> Death took on him the <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="Government">Goverment</span>, and was <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="Crowned">Crown'd</span> at <i>Kingston</i>. His lovely person <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="surnamed">sirnam'd</span> him the Fair, his actions are <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="diversely">diversly</span> reported, by <i>Huntingdon</i> not thought [illaudable](note:illaudable). But <i><span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="Malmesbury">Malmsbury</span></i> and such as follow him write [far otherwise](note:farotherwise), that he married or kept as Concubine, his <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="near">neer</span> [Kinswoman](note:kinswoman), some say both her and her Daughter; so <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="inordinately">inordinatly</span> <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="given">giv'n</span> to his pleasure, that on the very day of his Coronation, he abruptly withdrew himself from the Company of his Peers, whether in Banquet or Consultation, to sit wantoning in the Chamber with his <i>Algiva</i>, so was her name, who had such power over him. <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="Whereat">Wherat</span> his Barons offended, sent Bishop <i>Dunstan</i>, the boldest among them, to request his return: he going to the Chamber, not only interrupted his dalliance, and <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="rebuked">rebuk'd</span> the Lady, but <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="taking">takeing</span> him by the hand, between force and persuasion brought him back to his Nobles. The King highly <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="displeased">displeas'd</span>, and [instigated](note:956) perhaps by her who was so prevalent with him, not long after sent <i>Dunstan</i> into banishment, <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="caused">caus'd</span> his Monastery to be <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="rifled">rifl'd</span>, and became an Enemy to all Monks. <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="Whereupon">Wherupon</span> <i>Odo</i> Archbishop of <i>Canterbury</i> <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="pronounced">pronounc't</span> a separation or divorce of the King from <i>Algiva</i>. But that which most incited <i>William</i> of <i><span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="Malmesbury">Malmsbury</span></i> against him, he gave that Monastery to be dwelt in by secular <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="Priests">Preists</span>, or, to use his own phrase, made it a [stable of Clerks](note:stableofclerks); at length these affronts done to the Church were so resented by the people, that the <i>Mercians</i> and <i>Northumbrians</i> revolted from him, and set up <i>Edgar</i> [his Brother](note:hisbrother), leaving to <i>Edwi</i> the <i>West-Saxons</i> only, [bounded by the River](note:957) <i>Thames</i>; with <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="grief">greif</span> <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="whereof">wherof</span>, as is thought, he [soon after](note:soonafterended) ended his <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="days">daies</span>, and [was buried](note:958) at <i>Winchester</i>. Mean while <i>Elfsin</i> [Bishop of that place](note:thatplace) after the Death of <i>Odo</i>, ascending by Simony to the Chair of <i>Canterbury</i>, and going to <i>Rome</i> the same year for his Pall, was <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="frozen">froz'n</span> to Death in the Alps.
