### <i>Ecbert</i>.

</div>

[<i>Ecbert</i>](note:ecbertking) in full <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="height">highth</span> of glory, having now <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="enjoyed">enjoy'd</span> his Conquest <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="seven">seaven</span> <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="peaceful">peacefull</span> years, his victorious Army long since disbanded, and the exercise of <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="Arms">Armes</span> perhaps laid aside, the more was found unprovided against a sudden storm of <i>Danes</i> from the Sea, who landing in [the 32](note:the32). of his Reign, wasted [<i>Shepey</i>](note:thenextyearagain) in <i>Kent</i>. <i>Ecbert</i> the next year, gathering an Army, for he had heard of [<span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="their">thir</span> arrival](note:theirarrival) in 35 Ships, gave them <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="Battle">Battail</span> [by the River](note:bytheriver) <i>Carr</i> in <i>Dorsetshire</i>; the event <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="whereof">wherof</span> was, that the <i>Danes</i> kept <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="their">thir</span> ground, and <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="encamped">encampt</span> where the field was fought; two <i>Saxon</i> Leaders, <i>Dudda</i> and <i>Osmund</i>, and two Bishops, as [some say](note:somesaysay), were there slain. This was the only check of Fortune we read of, that <i>Ecbert</i> in all his time <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="received">receav'd</span>. For the <i>Danes</i> returning [two years after](note:2yearsafterafter) with a great Navy, and [joining Forces](note:joiningforces) with the <i>Cornish</i>, who had <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="entered">enterd</span> League with them, were overthrown and put to flight. Of these invasions against <i>Ecbert</i>, the <i>Danish</i> History is [not silent](note:notsilent); whether out of <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="their">thir</span> own Records or ours, may be justly doubted: for of these times at home, I find them in much uncertainty, and beholding rather to Out-landish Chronicles, <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="than">then</span> any Records of <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="their">thir</span> own. The Victor <i>Ecbert</i>, as one who had done enough, seasonably now, after prosperous success, the next [year with glory](note:yearwithglory) ended his days, and [was buried](note:wasburied) at <i>Winchester</i>.
