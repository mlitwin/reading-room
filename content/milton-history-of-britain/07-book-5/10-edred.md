### <i>Edred</i>.

</div>

[<i>EDred</i>](note:eadred) the third Brother of <i>Athelstan</i>, the Sons of <i>Edmund</i> being yet but <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="Children">Childern</span>, next <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="reigned">reign'd</span>, not degenerating from his worthy predecessors, and <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="Crowned">Crown'd</span> at <i>Kingston</i>. <i>Northumberland</i> he <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="thoroughly">throughly</span> <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="subdued">subdu'd</span>, the <i>Scots</i> without refusal swore him Allegiance; yet the <i>Northumbrians</i>, ever of <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="doubtful">doubtfull</span> Faith, soon after chose to themselves [one <i>Eric</i>](note:ericbloodaxe) a <i>Dane</i>. <i>Huntingdon</i> still haunts us with this <i>Anlaf</i> (of whom we gladly would have <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="been">bin</span> <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="rid">ridd</span>) and will have him before <i>Eric</i> <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="recalled">recall'd</span> once more and reign <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="four">fowr</span> years, [then again](note:950) put to his shifts. But <i>Edred</i> <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="entering">entring</span> into <i>Northumberland</i>, and [with spoils](note:withspoils) returning, <i>Eric</i> the King fell upon his rear. <i>Edred</i> turning about, both shook off the Enemy, and <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="prepared">prepar'd</span> to make a second inroad: which the <i>Northumbrians</i> dreading [rejected](note:dreading) <i>Eric</i>, slew <i>Amancus</i> the Son of <i>Anlaf</i>, and with many presents appeasing <i>Edred</i>, submitted again to his <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="Government">Goverment</span>; nor from that time had Kings, but were <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="governed">govern'd</span> by Earls, of whom [<i>Osulf</i>](note:osulf) was the first. About [this time](note:953) <i>Wulstan</i> Archbishop of <i>York</i>, <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="accused">accus'd</span> [to have slain](note:tohaveslain) certain men of <i>Thetford</i> in revenge of <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="their">thir</span> Abbot whom the Townsmen had slain, was committed by the King to close Custody; but soon after <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="enlarged">enlarg'd</span>, was <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="restored">restor'd</span> to his place. <i><span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="Malmesbury">Malmsbury</span></i> writes that his crime was to have <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="connived">conniv'd</span> at the revolt of his Countrymen: but King <i>Edred</i> [two years](note:955) after <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="sickening">sick'ning</span> in the <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="flower">flowr</span> of his youth, <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="died">dy'd</span> much lamented, [and was buried](note:edreddead) at <i>Winchester</i>.
