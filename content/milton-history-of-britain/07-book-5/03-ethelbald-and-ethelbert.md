### <i>Ethelbald</i>, and <i>Ethelbert</i>.

</div>

<i>Ethelbald</i>, [unnatural and disloyal](note:unnatural) to his Father, fell justly into another, though contrary sin, of too much love to his <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="Father&#x27;s">Fathers</span> Wife; and whom at first he <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="opposed">oppos'd</span> coming into the Land, her now unlawfully marrying, he takes into his Bed; but not long enjoying, <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="died">dy'd</span> at three <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="year&#x27;s">years</span> end, without doing aught more worthy to be <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="remembered">rememberd</span>; having <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="reigned">reign'd</span> [two years with](note:2yearswith) his Father, impiously usurping, and [three after](note:3after) him, as unworthily inheriting. And his hap was all that while to be unmolested with the <i>Danes</i>; not of Divine <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="favor">favour</span> doubtless, but to his greater condemnation, living the more securely his incestuous Life. [<i>Huntingdon</i>](note:buriedhuntingdon) on the other side much praises <i>Ethelbald</i>, and writes him buried at <i><span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="Sherburne">Sherburn</span></i>, with great sorrow of the people, who <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="missed">miss'd</span> him long after. <i><span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="Matthew">Mat.</span> <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="Westminster">West.</span></i> saith, that he repented of his incest with <i>Judith</i>, and <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="dismissed">dismiss'd</span> her: but <i>Asser</i> an Eye witness of those times, mentions no such thing.
