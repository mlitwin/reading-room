### <i>Edmund</i>.

</div>

[<i>EDmund</i>](note:edmund18) not above [18 years old](note:942) succeeded his Brother <i>Athelstan</i>, [in courage](note:incourage) not <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="inferior">inferiour</span>. For in the second of his Reign he <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="freed">free'd</span> <i>Mercia</i> of the <i>Danes</i> that <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="remained">remain'd</span> there, and took from them the <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="Cities">Citties</span> of <i>Lincoln</i>, <i>Nottingham</i>, <i>Stamford</i>, <i>Darby</i>, and <i><span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="Leicester">Leister</span></i>, where they were <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="placed">plac'd</span> by King <i>Edward</i>, but it seems gave not good proof of <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="their">thir</span> fidelity. [<i>Simeon</i> writes](note:simeonwritesagain) that <i>Anlaf</i> setting forth from <i><span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="York">Yorke</span></i>, and having wasted Southward as far as <i>Northampton</i>, was met by <i>Edmund</i> at <i><span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="Leicester">Leister</span></i>; but that ere the <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="Battles">Battails</span> <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="joined">join'd</span>, peace was made between them by <i>Odo</i> and <i>Wulstan</i> the two Archbishops, with conversion of <i>Anlaf</i>; for the same year <i>Edmund</i> receav'd at the Font-stone this or another <i>Anlaf</i>, as saith <i>Huntingdon</i>, not him <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="spoken">spok'n</span> of before, who <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="died">dy'd</span> this year (so uncertain they are in the story of these times also) and held <i>Reginald</i> another King of the <i>Northumbers</i>, while the Bishop <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="confirmed">confirm'd</span> him; <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="their">thir</span> limits were divided North and South by <i>Watling-street</i>. But <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="spiritual">spirituall</span> kindred little <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="availed">avail'd</span> to keep peace between them, whoever gave the cause; for we read him [two years](note:944) after driving <i>Anlaf</i> (whom the Annals now [first call](note:firstcall) the Son of <i>Sitric</i> ) and <i>Suthfrid</i> Son of <i>Reginald</i> out of <i>Northumberland</i>, <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="taking">takeing</span> the whole Country into subjection. <i>Edmund</i> the [next year](note:945) <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="harrassed">haras'd</span> <i>Cumberland</i>, then gave it to <i>Malcolm</i> King of <i>Scots</i>, thereby bound [to assist](note:toassist) him in his Wars, both by Sea and Land; <i><span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="Matthew">Mat.</span> <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="Westminster">West.</span></i> [adds that](note:mataddsthat) in this action <i>Edmund</i> had the aid of <i>Leolin</i> Prince of <i>Northwales</i>, against <i>Dummail</i> the <i>Cumbrian</i> King, him depriving of his <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="Kingdom">Kingdome</span>, and his two Sons of <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="their">thir</span> sight. But the [year after](note:946) he himself by strange accident came to an untimely Death, [feasting](note:feasting) with his Nobles on <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="Saint">St.</span> <i><span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="Austin&#x27;s">Austins</span></i> Day at <i>Puclekerke</i> in <i><span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="Glocestershire">Glostershire</span></i>, to <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="celebrate">celebrat</span> the memory of his first converting the <i>Saxons</i>; he <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="spied">spi'd</span> <i>Leof</i> a noted <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="Thief">Theef</span>, whom he had <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="banished">banish'd</span>, sitting among his Guests: <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="whereat">wherat</span> transported with too much vehemence of Spirit, though in a just cause, <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="rising">riseing</span> from the Table he ran upon the <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="Thief">Theef</span>, and catching his hair, <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="pulled">pull'd</span> him to the ground. The <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="Thief">Theef</span> who doubted from such handling no less <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="than">then</span> his Death intended, thought to die not <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="unrevenged">unreveng'd</span>; and with a short Dagger <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="struck">strook</span> the King, who still laid at him, and little expected such assassination, mortally into the <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="breast">brest</span>. The matter was done in a moment, ere men set at Table could turn them, or <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="imagine">imagin</span> at first what the stir meant, till <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="perceiving">perceaving</span> the King deadly wounded, they flew upon the murderer and <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="hewed">hew'd</span> him to <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="pieces">peeces</span>; who like a wild Beast at <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="bay">abbay</span>, seeing himself surrounded, <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="desperately">desperatly</span> laid about him, wounding some in his fall. The King was buried at <i>Glaston</i>, <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="whereof">wherof</span> <i>Dunstan</i> was then Abbot, his Laws yet remain to be seen among the Laws of other <i>Saxon</i> Kings.
