### <i>Ethelbert</i> alone.

</div>

<i>Ethelbald</i> by Death <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="removed">remov'd</span>, the whole Kingdom came rightfully to <i>[Ethelbert](note:ethelbertalone)</i> his next Brother. Who though a Prince of great <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="Virtue">Vertue</span> and no blame, had as short a Reign allotted him as his faulty Brother, nor that so <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="peaceful">peacefull</span>; once or twice invaded by the <i>Danes</i>. But they having landed in the West with a great Army, and <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="sacked">sackt</span> <i>Winchester</i>, were met by <i>Osric</i> Earl of <i>Southampton</i>, and <i>Ethelwolf</i> of <i><span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="Berkshire">Barkshire</span></i>, <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="beaten">beat'n</span> to <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="their">thir</span> Ships, and <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="forced">forc't</span> to leave <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="their">thir</span> booty. [Five years after](note:5yearsafter), about the time of his Death, they [set foot](note:setfoot) again in <i><span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="Thanet">Tanet</span></i>; the Kentish men wearied out with so frequent Alarms, came to agreement with them for a certain sum of money; but ere the peace could be <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="ratified">ratifi'd</span>, and the money <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="gathered">gatherd</span>, the <i>Danes</i> impatient of delay by a sudden eruption in the night, soon wasted all the <i>East</i> of <i>Kent</i>. Mean while or something before, <i>Ethelbert</i> deceasing was buried as his Brother at <i>Sherburne</i>.
