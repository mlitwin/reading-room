### <i>Ethelwolf</i>.

</div>

<i>[Ethelwolf](note:aethelwolf)</i> the Son of <i>Ecbert</i> succeeded, by <i><span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="Malmesbury">Malmsbury</span></i> <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="described">describ'd</span> a man of mild nature, not <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="inclined">inclin'd</span> to War, or delighted with much Dominion; that <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="therefore">therfore</span> contented with the <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="ancient">antient</span> <i>West-Saxon</i> bounds, he gave to <i>Ethelstan</i> his Brother, [or Son](note:hegave2), as some write, the <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="Kingdom">Kingdome</span> of <i>Kent</i> and <i>Essex</i>. But the <i>Saxon</i> Annalist, whose <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="Authority">Autority</span> is Elder, saith plainly, that both these Countries and <i>Sussex</i>, were <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="bequeathed">bequeath'd</span> to <i>Ethelstan</i> by <i>Ecbert</i> his Father. The unwarlike disposition of <i>Ethelwolf</i>, gave encouragement no doubt, and easier entrance to the <i>Danes</i>, who [came again](note:cameagain) the next year with 33 Ships; but <i>Wulfheard</i>, one of the <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="King&#x27;s">Kings</span> Chief Captains, [drove them back](note:drovethemback) at <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="Southampton">Southamton</span> with great slaughter; himself dying the same year, of Age, as I suppose, for he seems to have <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="been">bin</span> one of <i><span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="Ecbert&#x27;s">Ecberts</span></i> old Commanders, who was sent with <i>Ethelwolf</i> to subdue <i>Kent</i>. <i>Ethelhelm</i> another of the <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="King&#x27;s">Kings</span> Captains, with the <i>Dorsetshire</i> men, had at first like success against the <i>Danes</i> at <i>Portsmouth</i>; but they reinforcing stood <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="their">thir</span> ground, and put the English to rout. Worse was the success of Earl <i>Herebert</i> at a place <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="called">call'd</span> <i>Mereswar</i>, slain with [the most part](note:themostpart) of his Army. The [year following](note:followingin) in <i>Lindsey</i> also, <i>East-Angles</i>, and <i>Kent</i>, [much mischief](note:muchmischief) was <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="done">don</span> by <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="their">thir</span> landing; where the next year, <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="emboldened">embold'nd</span> by success, [they came on](note:theycameon) as far as <i>Canterbury</i>, <i>Rochester</i>, and <i>London</i> it self, with no less cruel hostility: and giving no <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="respite">respit</span> to the peaceable mind of <i>Ethelwolf</i>, they yet <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="returned">return'd</span> with the next year [in 35 Ships](note:in35ships), fought with him, [as before](note:asbefore) with his Father at the River <i>Carr</i>, and made good <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="their">thir</span> ground. In <i>Northumberland</i>, <i>Eandred</i> the Tributary King deceasing, [left the same](note:leftthesame) tenure to his Son <i>Ethelred</i> <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="driven">driv'n</span> out in his <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="fourth">fowrth</span> year, and succeeded by <i>Readwulf</i>, who [soon after](note:soonafter) his Coronation hasting forth to <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="Battle">Battel</span> against the <i>Danes</i> at <i>Alvetheli</i>, fell with the most part of his Army; and <i>Ethelred</i> like in fortune to the former <i>Ethelred</i>, was re-exalted to his Seat. And to be yet further like him in Fate, was slain the <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="fourth">fowrth</span> year after. <i>Osbert</i> succeeded in his room. But [more southerly](note:moresoutherly), the <i>Danes</i> next year after met with [some stop](note:somestop) in the full course of <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="their">thir</span> <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="outrageous">outragious</span> insolences. For <i>Earnulf</i> with the men of <i>Somerset</i>, <i>Alstan</i> the Bishop, and <i>Osric</i> with those of <i>Dorsetshire</i>, setting upon them at the <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="River&#x27;s">Rivers</span> mouth of <i>Pedridan</i>, <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="slaughtered">slaughterd</span> them in great numbers, and <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="obtained">obtain'd</span> a just Victory. This repulse <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="quelled">queld</span> them, for <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="aught">ought</span> we hear, the space [of six years](note:ofsixyears); Then also renewing <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="their">thir</span>, invasion with little better success. For <i>Keorle</i> an Earl, aided with the Forces of <i>Devonshire</i>, assaulted and [over-threw them](note:littlebetter) at <i>Wigganbeorch</i> with great destruction; as prosperously were they fought with the same year at <i>Sandwich</i>, by King <i>Ethelstan</i>, and <i>Ealker</i> his General, <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="their">thir</span> great Army defeated, and nine of <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="their">thir</span> Ships <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="taken">tak'n</span>, the rest <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="driven">driv'n</span> off; however to ride out the Winter on that <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="shore">shoar</span>, <i>Asser</i> saith, they then first <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="wintered">winter'd</span> in <i>Shepey <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="Isle">Ile</span></i>. Hard it is, through the bad expression of these Writers, to define this fight, whether by Sea or Land; <i>Hoveden</i> terms it [a Sea fight](note:aseafight). Nevertheless with 50 Ships (<i>Asser</i> [and others](note:andotherssay) add 300) they <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="entered">enterd</span> the mouth of <i>Thames</i>, and made excursions as far as <i>Canterbury</i> and <i>London</i>, and as <i>Ethelwerd</i> writes, <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="destroyed">destroy'd</span> both; of <i>London</i>, <i>Asser</i> signifies only that they <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="pillaged">pillag'd</span> it. <i>Bertulf</i> also the <i>Mercian</i>, successor of <i>Withlaf</i>, with all his Army they <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="forced">forc'd</span> to fly, and him beyond the Sea. Then passing over <i>Thames</i> with <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="their">thir</span> powers into <i>Surrey</i>, and the <i>West-Saxons</i>, and meeting there with King <i>Ethelwolf</i> and <i>Ethelbald</i> his Son, at a place <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="called">call'd</span> <i>Ak-Lea</i>, or <i>Oak-Lea</i>, they <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="received">receav'd</span> a total defeat with memorable slaughter. This was counted a [lucky year](note:luckyyear) to <i>England</i>, and brought to <i>Ethelwolf</i> great reputation. <i>Burhed</i> <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="therefore">therfore</span>, who after <i>Bertulf</i> [held of him](note:heldofhim) the <i>Mercian</i> Kingdom, two years after this, imploring his aid against the <i><span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="North-Welsh">North-Welch</span></i>, as then troublesome to his Confines, <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="obtained">obtain'd</span> it of him in person, and <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="thereby">therby</span> <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="reduced">reduc'd</span> them to obedience. This done, <i>Ethelwolf</i> sent his Son <i>Alfrid</i> a Child of five years, well accompanied to <i>Rome</i>, whom <i>Leo</i> the Pope both consecrated to be King afterward, and adopted to be his Son; at home <i>Ealker</i> with the Forces of <i>Kent</i>, and <i>Huda</i> with those of <i>Surrey</i>, fell on the <i>Danes</i> at <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="their">thir</span> landing in <i><span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="Thanet">Tanet</span></i>, and at first put them back; but the slain and <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="drowned">drown'd</span> were at length so many on either side, as left the loss equal on both: which yet <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="hindered">hinderd</span> not the solemnity of a marriage [at the feast](note:atthefeast) of <i>Easter</i>, between <i>Burhed</i> the <i>Mercian</i>, and <i>Ethelswida</i> King <i>Ethelwolf's</i> Daughter. Howbeit the <i>Danes</i> the [next year](note:thenextnextyear) [<span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="wintered">winterd</span> again](note:winterdagainagain) in <i>Shepey</i>. Whom <i>Ethelwolf</i>, not finding human health sufficient to resist, growing daily upon him, in hope of divine aid, <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="registered">registerd</span> in a Book and dedicated to God the tenth part of his own lands, and of his whole <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="Kingdom">Kingdome</span>, <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="eased">eas'd</span> of all impositions, but converted to the maintenance of Masses and Psalms weekly to be sung for the prospering of <i>Ethelwolf</i> and his Captains, as appears at large by [the Patent](note:thepatent) it self, in <i>William</i> of <i><span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="Malmesbury">Malmsbury</span></i>. <i>Asser</i> saith, he did it for the <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="redemption">redemtion</span> of his Soul and the Soul of his Ancestors. After which, as having done some great matter to <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="show">shew</span> himself at <i>Rome</i>, and be applauded of the Pope; he takes a long and cumbersome journey <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="thither">thether</span> with young <i>Alfrid</i> again, and there [<span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="stays">staies</span> a year](note:staysayear), when his place <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="required">requir'd</span> him rather <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="here">heer</span> [in the field](note:inthefield) against [Pagan Enemies](note:paganenemies) left <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="wintering">wintring</span> in his land. Yet so much manhood he had, as to return thence no Monk; and in his way home [took to Wife](note:tooktowife) <i>Judith</i> Daughter of <i>Charles</i> the bald, King of <i>France</i>. But ere his return, <i>Ethelbald</i> his eldest Son, <i>Alstan</i> his trusty Bishop, and <i>Enulf</i> Earl of <i>Somerset</i> <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="conspired">conspir'd</span> against him; <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="their">thir</span> complaints were, that he had <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="taken">tak'n</span> with him <i>Alfrid</i> his youngest Son to be there inaugurated King, and brought home with him an out-landish Wife; for which they <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="endeavored">endeavourd</span> to deprive him of his Kingdom. The disturbance was expected to bring forth nothing less <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="than">then</span> War: but the King abhorring civil discord, after many conferences tending to peace, condescended to divide the Kingdom with his Son; division was made, but the matter so carried, that the Eastern and worst part was malignly afforded to the Father; the Western and best <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="given">giv'n</span> to the Son: at which many of the Nobles had great indignation, <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="offering">offring</span> to the King <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="their">thir</span> utmost assistance for the recovery of all; whom he peacefully dissuading, sat down contented with his portion <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="assigned">assign'd</span>. In the <i>East-Angles</i>, <i>Edmund</i> lineal from the <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="ancient">antient</span> stock of those Kings, a youth of 14 years only, but of great hopes, was with [consent of all](note:consentofall2) but his own <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="Crowned">Crown'd</span> at <i><span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="Bury">Burie</span></i>. About this time, as <i>[Buchanan](note:buchrelates)</i> relates, the <i>Picts</i>, who not long before had by the <i>Scots</i> <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="been">bin</span> <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="driven">driv'n</span> out of <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="their">thir</span> <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="Country">Countrey</span>, part of them coming to <i>Osbert</i> and <i>Ella</i>, then Kings of <i>Northumberland</i>, <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="obtained">obtain'd</span> aid against <i>Donaldus</i> the <i><span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="Scottish">Scotish</span></i> King, to recover <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="their">thir</span> <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="ancient">antient</span> possession. <i>Osbert</i> who in person undertook the expedition, marching into <i>Scotland</i> was at first put to a retreat; but returning soon after on the <i>Scots</i>, over-secure of their <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="supposed">suppos'd</span> Victory, put them to flight with great slaughter, took <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="Prisoner">Pris'ner</span> <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="their">thir</span> King, and <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="pursued">persu'd</span> his Victory beyond <i><span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="Stirling-bridge">Stirlinbridge</span></i>. The <i>Scots</i> unable to resist longer, and by <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="Ambassadors">Embassadors</span> entreating peace, had it granted them on these conditions: The <i>Scots</i> were to quit all they had <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="possessed">possess'd</span> within the Wall of <i>Severus</i>: the limits of <i>Scotland</i> were beneath <i><span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="Stirling-bridge">Stirlinbridge</span></i> to be the River <i>Forth</i>, and on the other side, <i><span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="Dunbarton">Dunbritton</span> <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="Firth">Frith</span></i>; from that time so <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="called">call'd</span> of the <i><span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="British">Brittish</span></i> then seated in <i>Cumberland</i>, who had <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="joined">joind</span> with <i>Osbert</i> in this Action, and so far extended on that side the <i><span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="British">Brittish</span></i> limits. If this be true, as the <i>Scotch</i> Writers <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="themselves">themselv's</span> witness (and who would think them Fabulous to the disparagement of <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="their">thir</span> own Country?) how much wanting have <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="been">bin</span> our Historians to <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="their">thir</span> <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="Country&#x27;s">Countries</span> <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="Honor">Honour</span>, in <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="letting">leting</span> pass <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="unmentioned">unmention'd</span> an exploit so memorable, by them <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="remembered">rememberd</span> and attested, who are wont <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="oftener">ofter</span> to extenuate <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="than">then</span> to <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="amplify">amplifie</span> aught done in <i>Scotland</i> by the English? <i>Donaldus</i> on these conditions <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="released">releas't</span>, soon after <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="dies">dyes</span>, according to <i>Buchanan</i>, in 858. <i>Ethelwolf</i> Chief King in <i>England</i>, had the year before ended his life, and was buried as his Father at <i>Winchester</i>. He was from his youth much [addicted to devotion](note:addicted); so that in his <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="Father&#x27;s">Fathers</span> time he was <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="ordained">ordain'd</span> Bishop of <i>Winchester</i>; and unwillingly, for want of other Legitimate Issue, succeeded him in the Throne; managing <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="therefore">therfore</span> his greatest affairs by the activity of [two Bishops](note:2bishops), <i>Alstan</i> of <i>Sherburne</i>, and <i>Swithine</i> of <i>Winchester</i>. But <i>Alstan</i> is noted of Covetousness and Oppression, by <i>William</i> of <i><span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="Malmesbury">Malmsbury</span></i>; the more vehemently no doubt for doing some [notable damage](note:notabledamage) to that Monastery. The same Author writes, that <i>Ethelwolf</i> at <i>Rome</i>, paid a Tribute to the Pope, <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="continued">continu'd</span> to his <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="days">dayes</span>. However he were <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="facile">facil</span> to his Son, and seditious Nobles, in <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="yielding">yeilding</span> up part of his <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="Kingdom">Kingdome</span>, yet his Queen he treated not the less <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="honorably">honourably</span>, for whomsoever it <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="displeased">displeas'd</span>. The <i>West-Saxons</i> [had decreed](note:haddecreed) ever since the time of <i>Eadburga</i>, the infamous Wife of <i>Birthric</i>, that no Queen should sit in State with the King, or be <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="dignified">dignifi'd</span> with the Title of Queen. But <i>Ethelwolf</i> permitted not that <i>Judith</i> his Queen should <span class="inline-gloss" data-gloss="lose">loose</span> any point of Regal State by that Law. At his Death, he divided the Kingdom between his two Sons, <i>Ethelbald</i> and <i>Ethelbert</i>; to the younger <i>Kent</i>, <i>Essex</i>, <i>Surrey</i>, <i>Sussex</i>, to the elder all the rest; to <i>Peter</i> and <i>Paul</i> certain revenues yearly, for what uses let others relate, who write also his Pedigree, from Son to Father, up to <i>Adam</i>.
