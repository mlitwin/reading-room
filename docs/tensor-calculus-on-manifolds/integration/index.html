Integration

Matthew Litwin 2026-06-23

Integration on manifolds acts on top-degree forms over oriented manifolds. A metric adds a canonical top form, the volume form, so functions can be integrated too. The capstone is , ∫Mdω=∫∂Mω\int_M d\tens{\omega} = \int_{\partial M} \tens{\omega}∫M​dω=∫∂M​ω, which contains the fundamental theorem of calculus, Green’s, Gauss’s and the classical Stokes theorems.

Three pages:

  1. Orientation and integration: orientations, integrals of nnn-forms, change of variables.
  2. Volume form and Hodge star: volg\mathrm{vol}_gvolg​, integrals of functions, divergence, the area of S2S^2S2, the star and vector calculus.
  3. Stokes’s theorem: manifolds with boundary, the theorem, the divergence theorem.

Metric-free: orientation, integration of forms and Stokes’s theorem. The volume form, divergence and Hodge star need g\tens{g}g.

Where this lands in GR. The volume form makes the Einstein–Hilbert action ∫M(R−2Λ) volg\int_M (R - 2\Lambda)\, \mathrm{vol}_g∫M​(R−2Λ)volg​ a well-defined number. Stokes’s theorem is behind the integration by parts in every variational derivation and behind conserved-charge integrals over spatial slices.

Stokes's theorem

For an nnn-manifold MMM with boundary and a compactly supported (n−1)(n-1)(n−1)-form ω\tens{\omega}ω,

∫Mdω=∫∂Mω,\int_M d\tens{\omega} = \int_{\partial M} \tens{\omega}, ∫M​dω=∫∂M​ω,

with ∂M\partial M∂M given the outward-normal-first orientation. It contains the fundamental theorem of calculus, Green’s theorem, the classical Stokes theorem, and the divergence theorem. It also underlies integration by parts in every variational derivation. Defined on the Stokes page.

Orientation

An orientation of an nnn-manifold is a nowhere-vanishing nnn-form, taken up to multiplication by a positive function. A chart is positively oriented when dx1∧⋯∧dxndx^1 \wedge \cdots \wedge dx^ndx1∧⋯∧dxn agrees with it. An orientation is what makes ∫Mω\int_M \tens{\omega}∫M​ω of an nnn-form well-defined: the change-of-variables Jacobian then always has positive sign. The Möbius strip and Klein bottle admit none.
