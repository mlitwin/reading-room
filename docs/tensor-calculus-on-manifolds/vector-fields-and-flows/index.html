Vector fields and flows

Matthew Litwin 2026-06-23

A vector field is a smoothly varying choice of tangent vector at every point. This chapter covers their integral curves and flows and two derivative operators: the Lie bracket of two vector fields, and the Lie derivative along one.

Where this lands in GR. A flow is a one-parameter family of transformations. A flow that preserves the metric has a Killing vector as generator (£ξg=0\Lie_{\tens{\xi}}\tens{g} = 0£ξ​g=0), and by Noether’s theorem (chapter 12) each Killing field gives every free-falling particle a conserved quantity. The energy and angular momentum of Schwarzschild orbits are two of them.
