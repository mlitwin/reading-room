Differential forms

Matthew Litwin 2026-06-23

Differential forms are the algebraic objects you integrate over manifolds. The three pieces of the algebra — the wedge product, the exterior derivative, and pullback — combine to give Stokes’s theorem its full generality and to give de Rham cohomology its definition.

Where this lands in GR. The electromagnetic field strength FFF is a 2-form, and dF=0dF = 0dF=0 is half of Maxwell’s equations; “closed but not exact” is where gauge topology lives. Forms also carry the volume element of the action integrals in chapter 10 and the structure equations of Einstein–Cartan gravity.
