Special relativity

Matthew Litwin 2026-06-23

Special relativity is the natural-system picture of chapter 13 with configuration space replaced by spacetime. The kinetic metric becomes the flat Lorentzian metric η\tens{\eta}η, and Newton’s absolute time becomes one coordinate among four. It is the flat case that general relativity curves. Each tangent space of a curved spacetime is a copy of it (the Part I aside).

Two pages:

  1. Minkowski spacetime: the metric, Lorentz and Poincaré transformations, proper time, four-velocity and four-momentum.
  2. The relativistic particle: the proper-time action, reparametrization invariance, the mass shell, coupling to electromagnetism, and the Newtonian limit.

Where this lands in GR. Replace η\tens{\eta}η by a curved g\tens{g}g. The free-particle action is unchanged in form, and its Euler–Lagrange equations become the geodesic equation.
