Fields and stress–energy

Matthew Litwin 2026-06-23

Matter in general relativity is described by fields on spacetime, not by finitely many particles. This chapter carries the Lagrangian machinery of Part III over to fields. The configuration “space” becomes a space of functions on MMM, the action becomes an integral over spacetime, and Noether charges become conserved currents. Its endpoint is the , the source on the right of the Einstein equations. The material is kept to what chapter 15 needs.

Two pages:

  1. Lagrangian field theory: actions on spacetime, the field Euler–Lagrange equations, the scalar and Maxwell fields, Noether currents.
