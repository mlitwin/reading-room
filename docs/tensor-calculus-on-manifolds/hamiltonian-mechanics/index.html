Hamiltonian mechanics

Matthew Litwin 2026-06-23

The Hamiltonian formulation trades the second-order Euler–Lagrange equations on TQTQTQ for a first-order system on phase space, the cotangent bundle T∗QT^*QT∗Q. The two are related by the Legendre transform. Phase space carries a canonical closed 2-form, the symplectic form ω=dθ\tens{\omega} = d\tens{\theta}ω=dθ, built from the tautological 1-form of Part I. , canonical transformations and Liouville’s theorem are all statements about it.
