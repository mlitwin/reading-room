Multilinear maps and rank

Matthew Litwin 2026-06-23

The single mathematical object behind every tensor in physics is the multilinear map.

Definition

An (r,s)(r, s)(r,s)-tensor at ppp is a multilinear map

T:Tp∗M×⋯×Tp∗M⏟r copies×TpM×⋯×TpM⏟s copies→R.\tens{T}: \underbrace{T^*_p M \times \cdots \times T^*_p M}_{r \text{ copies}} \times \underbrace{T_p M \times \cdots \times T_p M}_{s \text{ copies}} \to \mathbb{R}. T:r copiesTp∗​M×⋯×Tp∗​M​​×s copiesTp​M×⋯×Tp​M​​→R.

Tensor field

A tensor field of type (r,s)(r, s)(r,s) on a smooth manifold MMM is a smooth section of the bundle whose fiber at p∈Mp \in Mp∈M is

TpM⊗r⊗Tp∗M⊗s.T_p M^{\otimes r} \otimes T^*_p M^{\otimes s}. Tp​M⊗r⊗Tp∗​M⊗s.

Equivalently, a C∞(M)C^\infty(M)C∞(M)-multilinear map taking rrr smooth 111-forms and sss smooth vector fields and returning a smooth real-valued function. Vector fields are (1,0)(1, 0)(1,0)-tensor fields; 111-forms are (0,1)(0, 1)(0,1)-tensor fields; kkk-forms are alternating (0,k)(0, k)(0,k)-tensor fields; the metric is a symmetric (0,2)(0, 2)(0,2)-tensor field. Derivation

For the commutative algebra A=C∞(M)A = C^\infty(M)A=C∞(M) of smooth functions on a manifold, a derivation at p∈Mp \in Mp∈M is an R\mathbb{R}R-linear map D:A→RD: A \to \mathbb{R}D:A→R satisfying the Leibniz rule

Every derivation at ppp is determined by its values on a coordinate system around ppp — D(xi)D(x^i)D(xi) for the chart x=(x1,…,xn)x = (x^1, \ldots, x^n)x=(x1,…,xn) — and the space of derivations is an nnn-dimensional real vector space, isomorphic to TpMT_p MTp​M.

The derivation viewpoint generalizes: a derivation of AAA (with values in AAA) is a vector field; the Lie bracket of vector fields is the commutator of derivations.
